The Biggest Mistake w/ AI The biggest lie I have seen floating around this space is that AI can magically fix your trading. If you lack a strong trading foundation, then AI can't save your... Foundation Includes: Defining Market Context Building Trading Playbooks Building Execution Playbooks Having a strong Feedback loop These are all necessary if you want to make it in this industry. However, instead of building a strong foundation, the majority of people try to vibe-code their own trading bots without even having a profitable strategy themselves. That's the biggest mistake I see beginner traders make with AI. Those who should start building their own AI trading bots are the traders who already know the pieces that are necessary to do so. 1. Rules for identifying a setup 2. Rules for executing trades 3. Rules for closing trades 4. Rules for sizing into trades At the end of the day, there is no harm in testing out trading bots on a demo account and just messing around to gather data and be more familiar with them. Just don't expect these bots to make you a profitable trader if you aren't one.
