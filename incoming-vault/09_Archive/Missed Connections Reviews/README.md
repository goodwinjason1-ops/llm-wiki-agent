# Archived Missed Connections Reviews

22 daily reviews from **2026-07-09 to 2026-07-29**, moved here on 2026-07-30.

They are the output of the *old* `connection_illuminator.py`, which matched notes
on unfiltered shared vocabulary. Its suggestions were largely stopword collisions,
its top candidate was repeatedly a "Latest" note against seven dated copies of
itself, and it had no memory, so the `Next synthesis candidates` block was
byte-identical across 22 of 23 reviews. Across all of them, 89 suggestions
produced 2 acted-on links.

Archived rather than deleted, per the vault rule that nothing is destroyed. They
have evidential value: they are the record of *how* the feedback instrument
failed, which is the story [[synthesis-debt]] tells.

Every one of them was an orphan — nothing linked to them, and nothing ever would.
Keeping them in `04_Wiki/queries` meant a fifth of the wiki was disconnected
output from a broken tool, which distorted every graph measurement taken of the
synthesis layer.

Reviews written by the replacement engine go to `04_Wiki/queries` as before. If
that folder starts filling with near-identical files again, the instrument has
regressed — check it against [[connection-illumination]] before trusting it.
