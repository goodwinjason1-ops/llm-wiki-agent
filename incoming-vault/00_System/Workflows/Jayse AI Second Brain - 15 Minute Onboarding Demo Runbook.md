# Jayse AI Second Brain - 15 Minute Onboarding Demo Runbook

## One-line promise

Turn messy links, notes, transcripts, screenshots, and project folders into an AI-readable Obsidian vault that Ari can search, update, connect, and turn into next actions.

## Demo audience

- Jayse, to explain the system quickly to himself or collaborators.
- BuyerProof AU / Business Context Brain prospects.
- Friends or operators who understand the value of a practical AI memory layer.

## Demo setup checklist

- [ ] Obsidian desktop is installed.
- [ ] Vault is open at `C:/Users/Kidsg/Documents/AI Second Brain`.
- [ ] `index.md`, `log.md`, and `00_System/Dashboards/START HERE.md` are open or easy to access.
- [ ] Ari/Hermes Telegram session is available.
- [ ] Use non-sensitive sample material only; do not paste secrets, API keys, wallet seeds, passwords, or private client data.

## 15-minute demo script

### 0:00-2:00 — Show the problem

Say:

> Most useful business and trading knowledge gets scattered across X bookmarks, YouTube videos, Telegram notes, PDFs, chats, and folders. Normal AI chats forget it. This vault makes the knowledge durable and action-ready.

Show:

- `index.md`
- `00_System/Dashboards/AI Second Brain Dashboard.md`
- `log.md`

### 2:00-5:00 — Capture something messy

Use a simple example:

```text
cap https://example.com/article
Intent: review this and tell me if it is useful for BuyerProof / Quant Floor / personal productivity.
```

Explain that Ari turns raw capture into:

```text
raw evidence → source summary → project/wiki/workflow note → dashboard/log/handoff update
```

### 5:00-8:00 — Show the vault structure

Show the folders:

| Folder | Role |
|---|---|
| `00_System` | dashboards, workflows, scripts, templates, handoffs |
| `01_Inbox` | unprocessed captures |
| `02_Raw` | immutable source evidence |
| `03_Sources` | source summaries |
| `04_Wiki` | durable concepts/entities/queries |
| `05_Projects` | active systems and business projects |
| `07_Resources` | reusable references |

### 8:00-11:00 — Show self-evolving loops

Open:

- [[Fable Style Self-Evolving Obsidian Loop]]
- `00_System/Scripts/vault_loop_runner.py`
- [[Connection Illumination Dashboard]]

Say:

> The loop scans inbox items, updates connection suggestions, checks vault health, writes a loop report, and refreshes handoff context so the next AI session resumes cleanly.

### 11:00-13:00 — Show a project outcome

Pick one:

- [[AI Quant Trading Floor]]
- [[AI Quant Morning Brief - Latest]]
- [[GitHub Tool Staging - 2026-07-08]]
- BuyerProof AU dashboard/project note

Explain:

> The vault is not just storage. It becomes an operating system: dashboards, scripts, recurring jobs, evidence gates, and next actions.

### 13:00-15:00 — Close with the business value

Say:

> For a business, this becomes a Business Context Brain: the AI can understand the company, the offers, the customers, the documents, and the decision history. For trading, it becomes a research floor where agents can eventually trade with guardrails after evidence, risk limits, monitoring, and explicit approval.

## Follow-up offer / product framing

**Business Context Brain starter kit**

1. Gather documents, links, call notes, website copy, FAQs, offers, and competitor info.
2. Build Obsidian/Markdown vault.
3. Add dashboards and capture templates.
4. Add an AI operating layer that can answer questions, generate assets, and keep the context fresh.
5. Add safety rules for private information and human approval.

## Demo safety rules

- Use sample/non-sensitive data in demos.
- Do not show private Telegram threads, wallets, API keys, client secrets, or personal documents without preparation.
- Keep trading examples clearly labeled as research/backtest/paper unless a live scope is explicitly approved.
