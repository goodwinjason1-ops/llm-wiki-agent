## Caveats

- This test uses **summary statistics** (hit rates, mean nets) from the V07 forward test. A full RST with raw net returns would be more precise.
- The binomial test on hit rates is a **directional test**: it asks whether the observed proportion of positive/negative returns exceeds 50% by chance.
- Multiple testing across 10 coins × 2 sides × 3 horizons = 60 tests inflates the family-wise error rate. Consider Bonferroni correction (α/60 ≈ 0.00083) for strict inference.
- **Does not replace** jitter robustness, paper-forward agreement, or cross-venue validation.
- The V07 funding proxy (`funding_rate × horizon_days`) is an approximation, not executed settlement data.
