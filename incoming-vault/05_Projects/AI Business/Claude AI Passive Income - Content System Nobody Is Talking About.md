## Key Insights

### 1. Evergreen content compounds
Unlike YouTube videos that decay after the algorithm stops pushing them, **search-optimized text content** (blogs, guides, tutorials) keeps earning for years. The teacher stopped making content but her library still generates income.

### 2. Claude eliminates the content bottleneck
The nurse had domain expertise but zero writing/SEO skills. Claude bridged the gap — she provided the raw knowledge, Claude structured it into optimized content.

### 3. Everyone has a niche
"I don't have a niche" = you haven't looked at what you already know. Your job, your hobby, your life experience — all niches. The nurse's niche was nursing education.

### 4. Low-tech, high-ROI
You don't need: fancy cameras, editing software, video production skills, or an audience. Just: Claude + a blog/Medium + basic SEO knowledge.
