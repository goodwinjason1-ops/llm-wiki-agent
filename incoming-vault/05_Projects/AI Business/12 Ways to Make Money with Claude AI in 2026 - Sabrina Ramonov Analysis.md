## Key Strategic Takeaways

### The leverage vs. speed tradeoff
- **Fast money = low leverage** (coaching, consulting). You trade time for cash.
- **High leverage = slow start** (courses, community, product). Build once, sell many.
- **Sweet spot for beginners:** Paths 1-3 (coaching, training, courses) get money flowing within weeks.

### Distribution patterns
- **Instagram & Facebook** are the "new LinkedIn" — people spend money there, DM automation works
- **Go hyper-local** for zero competition on consulting/training (Chamber of Commerce, SBA, local FB groups)
- **TikTok** is great for product virality, terrible for direct business/coaching sales

### The community math
- $50/member × 200 members = **$10,000/month recurring**
- You only need 200 people out of Claude's hundreds of millions of users
- Main risk: churn after 2-3 months — solve with ongoing value and engagement

### Why product wins long-term
- Adding 100 more customers requires **zero additional work** from you
- Recurring revenue + enterprise value (sell for 5-20x revenue)
- But: slowest path to first dollar, don't start here if you need money now
