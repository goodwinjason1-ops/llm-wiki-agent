# Approved direction

The current website visual direction is approved. The next pass should preserve the calm, editorial, evidence-oriented style and add interaction without turning the site into a generic SaaS dashboard.

## How It Works page

Convert the numbered five-step process into accessible clickable disclosure cards or an accordion:

1. **Discover** — clarify the decision, audience and constraints.
2. **Inventory** — gather the source material, systems and existing workflows.
3. **Map** — organise the facts, relationships, gaps and decision points.
4. **Implement** — produce the practical artefact, workflow, content or operating system.
5. **Review** — check what changed, what was used and what should improve next.

Each expanded step should contain:

- A short explanation
- A representative output
- A small example
- A relevant example from at least one of three business shapes:
  - consulting business
  - trade/supply business
  - community business

## Three demonstrations

Flesh out the existing demonstrations on the How It Works page. They should remain examples, not unsupported client claims:

- Consulting: scattered notes, proposals and client knowledge → reusable context and a clearer decision/follow-up system.
- Trade/supply: product lists, quote conversations and handover notes → quote/SOP intelligence and a repeatable enquiry workflow.
- Community: program information, schedules, stakeholder notes and outcome records → an accessible operating map and review-ready reporting structure.

Potential pilots can later replace or extend these examples once scope, permission and evidence are documented.

## Homepage interaction

Make the three-part “messy sources → structured context → clear decisions” block clickable or disclosure-based:

- **Messy sources:** inboxes, PDFs, spreadsheets, notes, conversations and disconnected tools.
- **Structured context:** a maintained map of people, services, products, evidence, workflows and constraints.
- **Clear decisions:** what to prioritise, what to publish, what to fix, who owns the next action and how to review it.

## Visual refinement

- Increase contrast between the body background, section surfaces and headings so content does not bleed together.
- Make the “Source-to-System Studio” wordmark slightly larger and more prominent than the navigation labels.
- Keep the restrained teal, charcoal and warm off-white palette.
- Keep keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support and mobile usability.
