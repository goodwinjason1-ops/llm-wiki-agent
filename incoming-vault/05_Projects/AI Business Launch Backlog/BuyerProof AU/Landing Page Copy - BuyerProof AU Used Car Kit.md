### Body

Buying a used car is stressful because you are trying to judge the seller, the paperwork, the vehicle condition, the test drive, the service history, and the price — often in one short inspection.

Miss one red flag and you could be stuck with:

- finance owing,
- written-off or stolen history,
- odometer concerns,
- hidden accident damage,
- overdue servicing,
- expensive tyres/brakes/repairs,
- a car that looked fine for ten minutes but fails later.

BuyerProof AU gives you a simple structure before you inspect, message, test drive, negotiate, or pay a deposit.

## FAQ

### Does this replace a mechanic inspection?

No. This is not a mechanical inspection. It helps you prepare, spot obvious red flags, ask better questions, and organise information before deciding whether to proceed or book a qualified mechanic.

### Is this Australian-specific?

Yes. It is written for Australian buyers and includes reminders for PPSR, rego/VIN matching, service history, seller questions, and pre-purchase inspections.

### Can this guarantee I will not buy a bad car?

No checklist can guarantee that. The goal is to reduce avoidable mistakes and help you know when to slow down, ask more questions, or get professional help.

### Can mechanics/detailers use this as a lead magnet?

Yes. A co-branded version can be created for mechanics, mobile inspectors, and detailers who want to attract used-car buyers.
