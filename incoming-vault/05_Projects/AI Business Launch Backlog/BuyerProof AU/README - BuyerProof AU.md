# BuyerProof AU

## Positioning

**BuyerProof AU** is a family of practical Australian decision kits for expensive life-admin moments.

> Practical checklists, trackers, scripts, and red-flag guides for Australians buying property, buying used cars, moving rentals, and comparing renovation quotes.

## Core promise

> Make expensive decisions less chaotic before you speak to the professional, seller, agent, mechanic, cleaner, broker, or tradie.

## What BuyerProof AU is *not*

BuyerProof AU is not:

- legal advice,
- financial advice,
- investment advice,
- tenancy advice,
- mechanical advice,
- building advice,
- a replacement for licensed professionals.

It is an educational preparation toolkit.

## Initial product ladder

| Product | Status | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit Australia | Week 1 MVP | Fastest validation product |
| Australian Property Due Diligence Kit | Week 2 MVP | Highest strategic upside |
| Rental Exit / Bond-Back Kit Australia | Week 3 MVP | Companion property/moving product |
| Renovation Quote Comparison Kit | Week 4 MVP | High-ticket B2B/consumer bridge |

## Week 1 focus

Build the **Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit Australia v1** and the first BuyerProof AU landing page copy.

Why used-car first:

- fast to understand: “Don’t buy a lemon.”
- fast to build,
- clear checks: PPSR, rego/VIN, service history, odometer, test drive, pre-purchase inspection,
- easy to create short-form content,
- can validate direct consumer demand and B2B partner interest with mechanics/detailers.

## Working offer

> Buying a used car in Australia? Use this checklist before you inspect, test drive, message the seller, or pay a deposit.
