# Consultant Audit - BuyerProof AU

## Verdict

BuyerProof AU is a coherent opportunity cluster. The strongest feature is not AI; it is structured decision support for expensive Australian consumer moments.

The project should avoid overbuilding four full products before validation. The fastest revenue path is either:

1. a polished Used-Car Buyer Kit tested DTC and with mechanics/detailers, or
2. a B2B co-branded lead magnet offer for mechanics, buyer’s agents, cleaners, or renovation contractors.

## Ranked roadmap

### 1. Polish Used-Car Kit into a shareable v1

Why: fastest to understand and easiest to validate.

Done means:

- PDF-ready markdown exists,
- spreadsheet tracker exists,
- seller scripts exist,
- landing page exists,
- safety review passes,
- 3 feedback requests sent.

Proof required:

- file paths,
- excerpts,
- feedback responses or sent-message log.

Failure mode: too much detail for ordinary buyers.

Stop/pivot condition: mechanics say it is misleading or users cannot understand it.

### 2. Get expert feedback before public claims

Why: credibility and safety.

Steps:

1. Send draft to mechanic/detailer.
2. Ask specific feedback questions.
3. Revise kit language.
4. Capture approach note.

Done means 3 feedback responses or 10 outreach attempts.

### 3. Build the property kit as strategic product, not first sales dependency

Why: higher upside but more regulated and trust-heavy.

Done means draft kit + professional feedback path, not aggressive public launch.

### 4. Test B2B co-branded lead magnet

Why: one B2B setup can beat many low-ticket sales.

Offer:

> “I’ll turn this into a co-branded buyer checklist that brings you warmer leads.”

Targets:

- mobile mechanics,
- detailers,
- building inspectors,
- buyer’s agents,
- end-of-lease cleaners.

### 5. Delay full renovation kit until one validation loop completes

Why: high-ticket and attractive, but another branch before validation risks dilution.

## Three things to stop/defer

1. Stop building broad landing pages before expert feedback.
2. Defer rental kit until used-car/property feedback shows the format works.
3. Defer paid ads or complex funnels until at least one kit has organic/partner signal.

## What a weaker model can execute next

A weaker model can:

- convert markdown to PDF layout,
- format CSVs,
- write outreach emails,
- make social posts,
- create landing page variants.

A stronger model is needed for:

- product sequencing,
- professional-boundary judgment,
- roadmap trade-offs,
- interpreting expert feedback.
