# Property Due Diligence Workflow - BuyerProof AU

## Insight

Australian property buyers need a structured due-diligence tracker before making an offer or going unconditional: contract/legal review, finance, building/pest, strata, council/zoning, insurance, and key dates.

## Why it matters

Property is the highest-upside BuyerProof AU product because the purchase is large, emotional, and complex.

## Product implication

The kit must include inspection-day checklist, due diligence tracker, agent question scripts, offer-readiness checklist, risk score, and professional handoff list.

## Outreach implication

Potential partners: buyer’s agents, brokers, conveyancers, building inspectors, pest inspectors.

## Safety note

Do not provide legal/financial/investment/building advice. Always refer to qualified professionals.
