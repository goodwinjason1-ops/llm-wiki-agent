# Safety and Professional Handoff Rules - BuyerProof AU

## Insight

BuyerProof AU must sell preparation and organisation, not professional advice.

## Why it matters

The product categories touch regulated/high-risk areas: cars, property, tenancy, renovation/building, finance, insurance, and contracts.

## Product implication

Every kit needs: disclaimer near top, no guarantees, professional handoff list, and final decision-support section that does not make decisions for the user.

## Outreach implication

Professional partners are more likely to support the product if it sends users to them rather than replacing them.
