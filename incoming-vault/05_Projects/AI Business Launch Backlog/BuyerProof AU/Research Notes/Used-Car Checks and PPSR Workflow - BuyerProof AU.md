# Used-Car Checks and PPSR Workflow - BuyerProof AU

## Insight

Used-car buyers need a repeatable pre-inspection workflow: request VIN/rego, run PPSR, verify registration details, inspect/service-history red flags, test drive, and decide whether to book a mechanic inspection.

## Why it matters

This is the fastest BuyerProof AU MVP because the pain is easy to understand: avoid buying a lemon or a car with hidden title/finance/history issues.

## Product implication

The kit must include PPSR/VIN tracker, seller scripts, inspection checklist, test-drive checklist, cost worksheet, and mechanic handoff questions.

## Outreach implication

Mobile mechanics and pre-purchase inspectors are strong co-branded lead magnet partners.
