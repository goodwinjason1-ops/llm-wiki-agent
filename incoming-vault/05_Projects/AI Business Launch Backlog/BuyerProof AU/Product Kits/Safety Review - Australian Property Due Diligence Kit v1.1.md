# Safety Review — Australian Property Due Diligence Kit v1.1

Scope: `Product Kits/Australian Property Due Diligence Kit v1.md` (v1.1), `Product Kits/Australian Property Due Diligence Kit v1.1 - Printable.html`, `Landing Page Copy - BuyerProof AU Property Due Diligence Kit.md`, `5 Launch Posts - Property Due Diligence Kit.md`, `Spreadsheets/Australian Property Due Diligence Tracker.csv`.

## Verdict

**Pass for feedback / draft sharing. Not yet final public/commercial publish.**

The kit is consistently framed as an educational preparation tool, hands every regulated or technical question to a named professional, marks state/territory-dependent items as verify locally, and the decision section does not tell the user what to decide.

## Checks completed

- [x] Educational-only disclaimer near the top of the kit, printable version, and landing page footer.
- [x] States it is not legal, financial, investment, building, pest, insurance, tax, or property advice.
- [x] Professional handoffs present and specific: conveyancer/solicitor, building inspector, pest inspector, broker/lender, insurer, strata search, council, accountant.
- [x] No guarantees of outcome, condition, value, insurability, or bond of any kind.
- [x] State/territory-dependent items (cooling-off, auction rules, duty, pool fencing, sale-type rules) are consistently marked "verify locally/professionally" — no specific state rules asserted anywhere.
- [x] Official sources are generic and safe: Moneysmart, state revenue office calculators, state/territory consumer affairs bodies (with a "names change — verify" hedge), NBN Co.
- [x] Final decision section shows status only; "Walk away" is tied to the user's own pre-written conditions, not the kit's judgement.
- [x] Slow-down triggers are explicitly framed as "get professional eyes on this", not as diagnoses ("not automatically a dealbreaker").
- [x] Inspection checklist opens with "You are not a building inspector" and marks electrical as professional-check-required.
- [x] No affiliate, referral, or commercial relationships present or implied.
- [x] Launch posts contain no advice claims; Post 5 explicitly says "This isn't advice on what to buy."

## Required changes (applied during this review)

- Landing page subheadline said "so nothing gets skipped" — implied a completeness guarantee. Changed to "so you can see what's been checked, and what hasn't". **Fixed 2026-07-07.**

## Risky wording to avoid in marketing

| Avoid | Risk | Safer replacement |
|---|---|---|
| "Complete due diligence" | implies nothing can be missed | "Due diligence preparation checklist" |
| "Know if a property is safe/sound" | building-advice claim | "Know what to hand to a licensed building inspector" |
| "Never get caught out at auction" | outcome guarantee | "Walk in with your checks tracked and your maximum written down" |
| "Covers everything you need to check" | completeness guarantee | "Covers the checks buyers most often skip" |
| "So nothing gets skipped" | completeness guarantee | "See what's been checked and what hasn't" (applied) |
