# BuyerProof AU - Brand and Offer Brief

## Brand idea

**BuyerProof AU** helps Australians prepare for expensive decisions with simple checklists, trackers, scripts, and red-flag guides.

## Tagline options

1. **Check before you commit.**
2. **Practical checklists for expensive decisions.**
3. **Red-flag kits for Australian buyers.**
4. **Make big purchases less risky.**
5. **Know what to check before you pay.**

Recommended first tagline:

> **Check before you commit.**

## Initial audience

### Primary

Australians making expensive one-off decisions:

- buying a used car,
- buying a home/investment property,
- moving out of a rental,
- comparing renovation quotes.

### Secondary B2B partners

Businesses that can use co-branded kits as lead magnets:

- mobile mechanics,
- pre-purchase car inspectors,
- car detailers,
- buyer’s agents,
- mortgage brokers,
- conveyancers,
- building/pest inspectors,
- end-of-lease cleaners,
- removalists,
- renovators/tradies.

## Tone

- Practical.
- Calm.
- Australian.
- Non-alarmist but red-flag aware.
- “Here’s what to ask/check” rather than “we know everything.”

## Core messaging

### Problem

Big purchases and move-out moments are stressful because the average person does not know what to check, what to ask, or what to record.

### Solution

BuyerProof AU gives them a structured checklist, tracker, scripts, and professional handoff notes before they commit money.

### Differentiator

Not generic blog advice. The kit is designed to be used while inspecting, messaging, comparing, and deciding.

## First product message

### Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit Australia

> Buying a used car privately or from a dealer? This kit helps you check the obvious red flags, ask the seller better questions, track PPSR/VIN/rego checks, compare repair costs, and prepare for a proper mechanic inspection.

## Safety/disclaimer language

Short version:

> BuyerProof AU kits are educational preparation tools only. They do not replace legal, financial, mechanical, building, tenancy, or professional advice. Always use qualified professionals for major decisions.

Used-car version:

> This kit is not a mechanical inspection and cannot guarantee a vehicle’s condition. Always consider a PPSR check, registration/VIN verification, and an independent pre-purchase inspection by a qualified mechanic before buying.

## Product ladder

| Tier | Offer | Price idea |
|---|---|---:|
| Free | Red-flag checklist PDF | Free |
| Starter | Full checklist + seller scripts | A$19 |

## Week 1 success target

Minimum:

- one usable kit,
- one landing page draft,
- five short-form posts,
- clear outreach list categories,
- ready to test with mechanics/detailers/car buyers.

Strong:

- first feedback from one mechanic/detailer/car enthusiast,
- first free download/waitlist page ready to publish.
