# BuyerProof AU Validation and Outreach Framework

## Objective

Validate whether BuyerProof AU kits are useful enough to download, buy, or co-brand with professional/service partners.

## Validation targets

### Used-car kit

- mobile mechanics,
- pre-purchase car inspectors,
- car detailers,
- driving instructors,
- parents/first-car buyers,
- car enthusiast community admins.

### Property kit

- buyer’s agents,
- mortgage brokers,
- conveyancers/solicitors,
- building inspectors,
- pest inspectors,
- property managers.

### Rental/bond kit

- end-of-lease cleaners,
- removalists,
- property managers,
- renters/student groups.

## Feedback questions — used-car kit

1. What is missing from this checklist that a buyer should know before inspection?
2. Which items are too technical or risky for a non-mechanic?
3. What wording should be softened to avoid pretending to be mechanical advice?
4. Would this help buyers come to you better prepared?
5. Would you use a co-branded version as a lead magnet?
6. What would make this worth A$19–A$39 to a buyer?
7. What would make a B2B version worth A$250–A$750 setup?

## Outreach script — mechanic/detailer feedback

```text
Hey [Name], I’m building BuyerProof AU — simple Australian checklists for expensive buying decisions.

The first one is a Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit. It helps buyers organise PPSR/VIN checks, seller questions, test-drive notes, obvious red flags, and questions to ask a qualified mechanic.

It does not replace a mechanic inspection — the goal is to help buyers arrive better prepared and know when to get professional help.

Would you be open to giving quick feedback on the draft? If useful, I can also make a co-branded version as a lead magnet for your business.
```

## Outreach script — property professionals

```text
Hey [Name], I’m building BuyerProof AU — practical Australian decision kits for expensive life-admin moments.

One kit is an Australian Property Due Diligence checklist for first-home buyers/investors. It is educational only and points buyers toward professionals like conveyancers, brokers, building/pest inspectors, and insurers.

Would you be open to giving quick feedback on whether the checklist helps buyers ask better questions without crossing into legal/financial/building advice?

If useful, I can create a co-branded lead magnet version for your clients/leads.
```

## Outreach script — cleaners/removalists

```text
Hey [Name], I’m building BuyerProof AU — practical Australian checklists for stressful life-admin moments.

One upcoming kit is a Rental Exit / Bond-Back checklist with room-by-room cleaning prompts, photo-proof tracker, and cleaner quote request template.

Would a co-branded version be useful as a lead magnet for renters moving out?
```

## Prospect tracker schema

Use `Outreach/BuyerProof AU Prospect Tracker.csv`.

Columns:

- Category
- Business/person
- Website/social
- Contact method
- Location
- Product fit
- Message sent date
- Response
- Feedback notes
- Follow-up date
- Co-brand interest
- Paid interest
- Next action

## Continue/pivot criteria

Continue a kit if one of these happens within 30 days:

- 50+ free downloads,
- 10+ paid purchases,
- 3+ expert feedback responses,
- 1+ B2B co-brand client,
- clear partner demand.

Pivot/rework if:

- nobody understands the offer,
- experts say it is unsafe/misleading,
- users only want free info with no partner interest,
- acquisition channel is too expensive or unclear.
