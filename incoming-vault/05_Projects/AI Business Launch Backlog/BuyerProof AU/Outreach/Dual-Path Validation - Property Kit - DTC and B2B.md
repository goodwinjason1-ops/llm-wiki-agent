# Dual-Path Validation — Property Due Diligence Kit

Two demand signals, one asset. The free inspection-day checklist is the shared unit; only the wrapper changes.

## Path A — DTC download

- **Asset:** free inspection-day checklist (extract from kit v1.1) behind an email signup.
- **Channel:** 5 launch posts (`5 Launch Posts - Property Due Diligence Kit.md`) + landing page.
- **Signal:** downloads and "PROPERTY" comments → then paid-kit conversion at A$29 vs A$49.
- **Pass bar (suggested):** 30 downloads or 3 paid sales in 2 weeks = keep investing.

## Path B — B2B co-branded lead magnet

- **Asset:** the *same* free checklist with a partner logo strip and "provided by [partner]" line — no new content.
- **Prospects:** mortgage brokers, buyer's agents, conveyancers, building/pest inspectors (see `Outreach/BuyerProof AU Prospect Tracker.csv`).
- **Pitch:** "A branded checklist your leads actually use before they buy — you give it away, it keeps your name in front of them until settlement."
- **Signal:** replies and yes/no from 10 outreach messages; 2 partners saying yes = the B2B path is real.
- **Safety note:** co-branded versions keep the full disclaimer; partners may not edit wording that touches regulated claims.

## How to run both without diluting effort

1. **One asset, two wrappers.** Never build separate content for each path — B2B gets the DTC checklist with a logo. If a partner asks for custom content, that is a paid engagement, not validation.
2. **Sequence inside the same fortnight.** Days 1–3: publish landing page + posts (DTC starts collecting passively). Days 3–10: send 10 B2B messages (active effort goes here, since B2B needs outreach and DTC doesn't).
3. **One metric per path, reviewed once.** Downloads/sales for A; partner yeses for B. Review both on day 14, not daily.
4. **Decision rule:** whichever path clears its bar first gets the next build cycle (paid-kit polish for A; co-branded template + partner onboarding one-pager for B). If both clear, B2B wins the tiebreak — one partner distributes to many buyers.
5. **If neither clears:** run the feedback questions from `Outreach/BuyerProof AU Validation and Outreach Framework.md` before building anything else.
