# Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit Australia v1

## Purpose

This kit helps Australian used-car buyers prepare before messaging a seller, inspecting a vehicle, taking a test drive, negotiating, or booking a pre-purchase mechanic inspection.

## Disclaimer

This kit is an educational preparation tool only. It is not mechanical advice and is not a substitute for an independent pre-purchase inspection by a qualified mechanic. It cannot guarantee vehicle condition, title status, safety, or value. Always verify PPSR, VIN/rego details, service history, and current state/territory requirements before buying.

## Official / professional checks to consider

Use current official sources for final checks. Good starting points:

- **PPSR** — Personal Property Securities Register: https://www.ppsr.gov.au/
- **State/territory registration check** — use your relevant road authority / transport department.
- **Pre-purchase mechanical inspection** — use a qualified independent mechanic or inspection service.
- **Insurance quote** — check whether the car can be insured at a price you can afford before buying.
- **Roadworthy / safety certificate requirements** — vary by state/territory and sale type, so verify locally.

Important: if any official check conflicts with this kit, trust the current official/professional source.

## Ask before travelling

- Can you send the VIN and registration number?
- Is the car currently registered and roadworthy?
- Is there any finance owing on the vehicle?
- Has it ever been written off, stolen, repaired after accident damage, or affected by flood/hail?
- Do you have full or partial service history?
- Are there any warning lights, mechanical issues, leaks, or known faults?
- Why are you selling?
- Are you comfortable with an independent pre-purchase inspection?
- Are both keys/remotes available?
- Are there any modifications? If yes, are they legal/engineered/insured?

## Checks to prepare

- PPSR search using VIN.
- Registration/vehicle details check for your state/territory.
- Market price comparison.
- Insurance quote estimate.
- Approximate cost of tyres, service, rego transfer, stamp duty, repairs.
- Mechanic/pre-purchase inspection availability.

- fresh paint hiding damage.

# 7. Test-drive checklist

Only test drive if insured/allowed and safe. Bring another person if possible.

| Test | What to notice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Steering | pulls left/right, vibration, clunks |  |
| Braking | straight stop, no pulsing/squeal |  |
| Acceleration | hesitation, smoke, misfire |  |
| Gearbox | smooth shifts, no slipping/clunks |  |
| Suspension | knocks over bumps |  |
| Temperature | stays normal |  |
| Reverse/parking | camera/sensors, clunks |  |
| Highway speed if safe | vibration, tracking, wind noise |  |
| Low-speed turns | clicking, binding, steering noise |  |

Test-drive red flags:

- overheating,
- smoke under acceleration,
- clunking gearbox,
- brake vibration,
- steering pull,
- warning lights appearing during drive,
- seller avoids letting you drive normally.

# 9. Mechanic inspection handoff

Ask the mechanic/pre-purchase inspector:

- Are there signs of previous accident repair?
- Are there oil/coolant/transmission leaks?
- Are there suspension, brake, steering, or tyre concerns?
- Are there diagnostic fault codes?
- Is the odometer/service history consistent?
- Are there signs of overheating?
- Are there upcoming major service items?
- What repairs are urgent vs later?
- Is the car safe and roadworthy from your inspection perspective?
- What would you estimate immediate repair costs to be?
