# Seller Message Scripts - Used Car Kit

## First enquiry

Hi [Name], is the [car/year/model] still available? I’m interested, but before arranging a time could you please send:

- VIN,
- registration number,
- current odometer,
- service history/logbook photos if available,
- any known issues,
- whether there is any finance owing,
- reason for selling.

Thanks.

## Inspection request

Thanks for that. I’d like to inspect it in daylight and take a short test drive if possible. Is [day/time] suitable?

Also, are you comfortable with me arranging an independent pre-purchase inspection if I decide to proceed?

## Service history request

Could you please send photos of the service history/logbook and any recent receipts? I’m mainly checking whether major services, tyres, brakes, battery, and fluids are up to date.

## PPSR/VIN request

Could you please confirm the VIN so I can do a PPSR check before viewing? I like to check finance owing/write-off/stolen status before travelling.

## After inspection — negotiation

Thanks for showing me the car. I like [positive point], but I’d need to factor in [tyres/service/repair/rego/inspection item]. Based on that, I’d be comfortable at $____, subject to PPSR and/or mechanic inspection.

## Mechanic inspection condition

I’m interested, but I’d only proceed subject to an independent pre-purchase inspection. If the inspection is clean, I’d be ready to move forward at the agreed price.

## Walk-away message

Thanks for your time today. I’m going to pass on this one because [reason]. Good luck with the sale.
