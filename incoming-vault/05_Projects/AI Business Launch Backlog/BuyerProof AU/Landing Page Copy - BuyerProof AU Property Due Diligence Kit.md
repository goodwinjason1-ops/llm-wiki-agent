## Problem section

### Heading

The most expensive purchase of your life happens on the seller's timeline.

### Body

Between the first inspection and an offer or auction, you're supposed to organise a contract review, building and pest inspections, a strata search, finance conditions, an insurance quote, comparable sales, and a maximum price — often in under two weeks, while an agent tells you other buyers are ready to go.

Skip one check and you can end up with:

- a contract condition you didn't understand,
- structural or termite problems found after it's too late,
- a special levy nobody mentioned,
- a property that's hard or expensive to insure,
- a "bargain" that cost more than the asking price once duty, fees, and repairs landed.

BuyerProof AU gives you one structure for the whole run-up: what to check, who verifies it, and what has to be done before you're offer-ready.

## FAQ

### Does this replace a conveyancer or building inspector?

No. Nothing here is legal, building, pest, financial, insurance, tax, or property advice. The kit helps you organise the professional checks, ask better questions, and track what's verified — the professionals do the actual verifying.

### Is this Australian-specific?

Yes. It's built around Australian steps: contract review by a conveyancer/solicitor, building and pest inspections, strata/body corporate searches, council and overlay checks, stamp/transfer duty, and auction vs private treaty preparation. Where rules vary by state/territory, the kit says so and tells you to verify locally.

### Does it tell me whether to buy?

No. It shows you what's checked, what's a concern, and what's still unknown — the decision, and the professional advice behind it, stays yours.

### Can brokers, buyer's agents, or conveyancers use this as a lead magnet?

Yes. A co-branded version of the free inspection-day checklist can be created for mortgage brokers, buyer's agents, conveyancers, and building/pest inspectors.
