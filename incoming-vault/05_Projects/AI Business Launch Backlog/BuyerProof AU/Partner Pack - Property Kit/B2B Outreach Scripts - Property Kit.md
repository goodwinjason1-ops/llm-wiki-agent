# B2B Outreach Scripts — Property Kit

## Initial email / LinkedIn DM

Subject: Branded checklist for your property buyer leads

Hi [Name],

I’m building BuyerProof AU — practical Australian checklists for people making expensive decisions.

I’ve made a one-page **Property Inspection-Day Red Flag Checklist** for buyers to use before they make an offer, bid, or go unconditional. It’s educational only, with clear professional handoff prompts to conveyancers, brokers, inspectors, insurers, and council/strata checks where relevant.

I’m looking for 2–3 property-adjacent partners to test a co-branded version.

For [business name], I think it could work as a lead magnet because buyers can use it at open homes, and your name stays attached when they realise they need [broker/conveyancer/inspection/buyer's agent] help.

Would you like me to send a quick sample with your logo/name mocked up?

— Jayse / BuyerProof AU

## Follow-up 1 — value angle

Hi [Name], just following up.

The angle is simple: a buyer-friendly checklist that does **not** pretend to replace professional advice. It helps them organise what is unknown, then points them toward the right professional.

For [partner type], the natural CTA is:

> [Book a finance check / Send us the contract before you sign / Book a pre-purchase inspection / Book a buyer strategy call]

Happy to send the sample if useful.

## Follow-up 2 — tiny pilot ask

Hi [Name], last nudge from me.

I’m only trying to validate whether this is useful for property-adjacent businesses. The pilot is small:

1. I create a co-branded checklist sample.
2. You share it with a few leads or via one post/email.
3. We track replies/downloads/bookings for 14 days.

No big commitment. If it’s not useful, we stop.

Would you like to be one of the pilot partners?

## Call script

1. "Who are your best-fit buyer leads right now?"
2. "At what point do buyers usually come to you too late?"
3. "What do buyers misunderstand before they make offers or bid?"
4. "Would a checklist help educate them before that point?"
5. "What CTA would you want at the bottom: call, booking link, email, contract upload, inspection quote?"
6. "Can we test it with one email/post and review after 14 days?"
7. "Any wording you would be uncomfortable with from a compliance/professional-boundary perspective?"
