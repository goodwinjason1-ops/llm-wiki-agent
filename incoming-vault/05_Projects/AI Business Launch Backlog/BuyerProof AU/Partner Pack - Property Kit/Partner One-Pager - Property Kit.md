# BuyerProof AU Property Checklist — Partner One-Pager

## The offer

Give your prospective buyers a practical Australian inspection-day checklist they can use before they make an offer, bid, or go unconditional.

Partner positioning:

> "A branded checklist your leads actually use before they buy — with your name in front of them when they need professional help."

## Who this is for

Best-fit partners:

| Partner type | Why it fits | Likely call-to-action |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage brokers | Buyers need finance clarity before offers/auctions | "Book a borrowing capacity / pre-approval check" |
| Buyer's agents | Buyers need structure before inspections | "Book a buyer strategy call" |
| Conveyancers/solicitors | Buyers need contract/title review before signing/bidding | "Send us the contract before you commit" |
| Building/pest inspectors | Buyers need inspection support and risk handoff | "Book a pre-purchase inspection" |
| Insurance brokers | Buyers need insurability/cost checks before committing | "Check cover before you sign" |

## What the partner gets during validation

- Co-branded one-page checklist PDF/HTML.
- Short intro copy they can email or post.
- Clear disclaimer/professional-boundary wording.
- Simple tracking sheet for downloads/replies/bookings.

## What the buyer gets

- Before-you-leave-home checklist.
- Inspection-day red-flag areas to photograph or ask about.
- Strata/unit/townhouse prompts.
- Agent questions.
- Pre-offer/pre-bid readiness prompts.
- Professional handoff reminders.

## Validation package

Pilot offer:

- **Free/low-cost pilot for first 2–3 partners** in exchange for feedback, distribution metrics, and permission to use anonymised learning.
- After validation: A$250–A$750 setup for branded lead magnet + A$100–A$300/mo for updates/reporting, or custom quote for content/funnel support.

## Safety position

This is an educational preparation checklist only. It does not give legal, financial, investment, building, pest, insurance, tax, or property advice. It helps buyers organise what to verify with qualified professionals.

Partners may add their logo, contact details, booking link, and short intro. Partners may **not** remove disclaimers or rewrite regulated/professional claims without review.

## Success metrics for the first 14 days

- 10 partner outreach messages sent.
- 2 partner yeses = B2B path validated enough to continue.
- For each partner pilot: track downloads, replies, bookings, and qualitative objections.
