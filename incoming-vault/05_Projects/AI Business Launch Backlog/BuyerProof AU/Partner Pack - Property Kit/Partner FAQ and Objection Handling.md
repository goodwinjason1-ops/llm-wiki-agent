# Partner FAQ and Objection Handling

## What is this?

A co-branded educational checklist for Australian property buyers. It helps buyers prepare for inspections and identify what needs professional follow-up before making offers, bidding, or going unconditional.

## Is this legal/financial/building advice?

No. The checklist explicitly says it is not legal, financial, investment, building, pest, insurance, tax, or property advice. It points buyers toward qualified professionals.

## Why would a partner use it?

Because buyers often need structure before they are ready to call. A practical checklist builds trust and keeps the partner visible when the buyer reaches the point of needing professional help.

## Will it replace our advice/service?

No. It is designed to create more informed leads, not replace professional work.

## Can we edit the wording?

You can edit branding, contact details, intro, and bio. You cannot change disclaimers, professional-boundary wording, or regulated claims without review.

## Can we add our logo?

Yes. The whole B2B validation path assumes a logo/name/contact wrapper around the same checklist.

## What if our compliance team has concerns?

Good. Send the concerns back. The checklist should be adjusted conservatively and remain educational-only.

## What does the pilot involve?

- BuyerProof AU creates the co-branded checklist.
- Partner shares it with a small audience or lead list.
- We track downloads/replies/bookings for 14 days.
- We decide whether to continue, stop, or turn it into a paid partner asset.

## What counts as success?

For validation: 2 partner yeses from 10 outreach messages, or one partner who shares the checklist and gets meaningful buyer engagement.

## Main objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have content." | Great — this is not replacing content. It is a practical handout buyers can use at inspections. |
| "We can't give advice." | Same. The checklist is deliberately not advice and points to professionals. |
| "Our buyers are already informed." | Then this acts as a trust-building confirmation and referral tool. |
| "We don't want custom work." | The pilot is intentionally one shared checklist with a logo/contact wrapper. |
| "Will this create liability?" | It should be reviewed conservatively; disclaimers stay in place; no guarantees or regulated claims are added. |
