# Partner Safety and Editing Rules

## Non-negotiable rule

The checklist is an educational preparation tool. It must not become advice, a guarantee, or a substitute for qualified professionals.

## Do not remove

- Educational-only disclaimer.
- Professional handoff list.
- State/territory verify-locally wording.
- No-guarantee wording.
- "Unknown is not a pass" / decision-support framing.

## Do not add

- Legal advice.
- Financial or investment advice.
- Building/pest diagnosis.
- Insurance/tax advice.
- State-specific process claims unless verified by a qualified source.
- Guarantees of safe purchase, better deal, defect detection, approval, settlement, or completeness.

## Partner-specific risky areas

| Partner type | Risk | Safe handoff language |
|---|---|---|
| Broker | credit/loan advice claims | "Check your finance position with your broker/lender." |
| Conveyancer | legal/contract advice claims | "Have your conveyancer/solicitor review the contract before you commit." |
| Building inspector | defect diagnosis claims | "Photograph concerns and hand them to a licensed inspector." |
| Buyer's agent | investment advice claims | "Use this to organise your checks before deciding." |
| Insurance broker | insurability claims | "Request a quote and verify cover before committing." |

## Approval checklist before distribution

- [ ] Disclaimer present.
- [ ] Partner intro/footer factual only.
- [ ] No guarantee/completeness claims.
- [ ] No "this replaces professional advice" implication.
- [ ] Version/date/source checklist shown.
- [ ] Contact/CTA correct.
