# B2B Partner Pack for Property Kit

## 1. Problem solved

The property kit had DTC assets and a dual-path validation note, but it lacked the practical B2B assets needed to test partner distribution without custom work.

## 2. Why it mattered

BuyerProof AU can validate faster if one partner distributes to many property buyers. But uncontrolled partner edits create safety/compliance risk.

## 3. Initial assumptions

The B2B path needed a new product.

## 4. Trap / hidden difficulty

Building custom partner content before validation would dilute effort and increase risk.

## 5. Why the obvious approach was insufficient

A bespoke broker/conveyancer/inspector funnel for each partner would look more impressive but would slow validation and make safety review harder.

## 6. Approach that actually worked

Use one shared lead magnet and two wrappers: DTC email download and B2B co-brand header/footer. Create partner rules that permit branding changes but lock regulated/professional wording.

## 7. Step-by-step method

1. Use the existing property lead magnet as the single source asset.
2. Create a partner one-pager explaining value and fit.
3. Create a co-brand wrapper template rather than a new checklist.
4. Write outreach/follow-up/call scripts.
5. Add FAQ and onboarding checklist.
6. Add safety/editing rules and scoring sheet.

## 8. Checks that proved it worked

- Every partner asset points back to the same source checklist.
- Partner edit permissions are explicit.
- Disclaimer/professional boundaries are preserved.
- The validation metric remains simple: 2 partner yeses from 10 messages.

## 9. Reusable rule for next time

For B2B validation, create one shared asset plus wrappers. Treat custom partner requests as paid work, not validation.

## 10. Prompt/instruction future models should use

> Create B2B validation assets around the existing lead magnet. Do not create new regulated content. Partners may edit logo/contact/CTA only; disclaimers and professional-boundary wording stay locked.
