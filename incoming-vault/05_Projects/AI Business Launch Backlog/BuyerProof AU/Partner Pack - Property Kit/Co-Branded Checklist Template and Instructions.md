## Partner may change

- Logo/name.
- Contact details.
- Booking link.
- One short factual intro.
- One short factual bio/footer.
- Visual colours/layout.

## Partner may not change without review

- Disclaimer wording.
- Professional handoff wording.
- State/territory verification wording.
- Any wording that touches contracts, finance, auctions, strata, building defects, pest, insurance, tax, zoning, cooling-off, or legal obligations.
- Any claim that implies completeness or guaranteed outcome.

## Forbidden partner claims

Do not allow:

- "This checklist covers everything."
- "Never miss a defect."
- "Avoid buying the wrong property."
- "Guarantee a safe purchase."
- "You do not need a conveyancer/building inspector."
- "We guarantee finance/approval/settlement."

## Safer wording

Use:

- "Prepare before you commit."
- "See what you've checked and what is still unknown."
- "Know what to hand to your conveyancer/building inspector/broker."
- "Slow down and verify before making a high-stakes decision."

## Version rule

Every co-branded version must state:

- source checklist version,
- date generated,
- partner name,
- whether wording has been changed.
