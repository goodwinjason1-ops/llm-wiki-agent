# BuyerProof AU Opportunity Selection Approach

## 1. Problem solved

We had to choose a fast-launch business direction after rejecting or deprioritising crowded AI receptionist / generic AI business ideas.

## 2. Why it mattered

Jayse wants something launchable within roughly 30 days, with real profit potential, Australian relevance, and room to scale. Choosing the wrong first niche could waste the first month building something crowded, vague, or hard to validate.

## 3. Initial assumptions

Initial assumptions were that AI-enabled services for local businesses, content repurposing, and creator digital-product packaging might be strong first moves.

## 4. Trap / hidden difficulty

The trap was that AI-aware audiences — AI YouTubers, small-business coaches, and automation buyers — already understand many of these tools and are saturated with similar offers. Another trap was starting with a full SaaS or AI receptionist instead of a sharper, lower-risk MVP.

## 5. Why the obvious approach was insufficient

The obvious approach was to sell AI services to businesses or creators. That was insufficient because it put us in crowded categories and required convincing already AI-aware buyers. It also risked building technical infrastructure before proving demand.

## 6. Approach that actually worked

The stronger pattern was to target high-stakes Australian consumer decisions where people need structured checklists, scripts, trackers, and professional handoffs. These are less about selling "AI" and more about reducing anxiety and organising decisions.

## 7. Step-by-step method

1. Assessed playlist/business ideas for speed, profitability, realism, and scalability.
2. Checked competition risk around tradie AI receptionist and generic AI offers.
3. Shifted from AI-native buyers to practical, less-AI-aware decision moments.
4. Grouped property, used-car, rental exit, and renovation quote ideas into one umbrella: BuyerProof AU.
5. Chose Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit as the fastest Week 1 MVP.
6. Kept Australian Property Due Diligence Kit as the best long-term strategic product.
7. Created Week 1 assets and now installed project operating standards and reusable skills.

## 8. Checks that proved it worked

- User explicitly liked property due diligence, used-car inspection, and rental cleaning checklist.
- Week 1 workspace and MVP files were created in the Obsidian vault.
- Used-car product has a clear hook: "Don’t buy a lemon."
- The product family has a coherent umbrella: expensive Australian decisions.

## 9. Reusable rule for next time

When choosing a fast-launch AI-assisted business, do not lead with AI. Lead with a painful decision, a clear buyer, a checklistable workflow, and a professional handoff boundary.

## 10. Prompt/instruction future models should use

```text
Evaluate this new BuyerProof AU product idea by asking:
1. What expensive/stressful decision does it help with?
2. What red flags or mistakes does it organise?
3. What professional advice boundary must it not cross?
4. Can it be delivered as checklist + tracker + scripts + handoff?
5. Can it be sold DTC and also used as a B2B lead magnet?
6. Is it launchable in one week as v1?
```
