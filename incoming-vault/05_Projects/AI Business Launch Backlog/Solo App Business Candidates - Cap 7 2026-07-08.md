# Solo App Business Candidates - Cap 7 2026-07-08

## Source

Cap 7: `How I Build Apps SOLO That ACTUALLY Make Money in 2026`.

## Fit

**Strong.** This supports Business Context Brain's commercial direction: pick validated pain, ship fast, and capture a narrow slice of an existing market rather than trying to invent a novel idea.

## Extracted business rules

1. Ideas are cheap; shipping and distribution matter.
2. Use validated demand sources such as YC Requests for Startups, existing revenue directories, competitor reviews, and obvious workflow pain.
3. Pick real human/business pain, not novelty.
4. Small solo products can win by taking a thin slice of an existing market.
5. Prefer web-first/B2B/prosumer workflows over low-purchasing-power consumer mobile ideas.

## Application to Business Context Brain

This source argues for making Business Context Brain **boringly useful**:

```text
messy inputs → clean context map → dashboard → reusable workflows → monthly maintenance
```

Avoid building a generic SaaS first. Sell a service/pilot, then productize repeated intake, extraction, dashboard, and maintenance patterns.
