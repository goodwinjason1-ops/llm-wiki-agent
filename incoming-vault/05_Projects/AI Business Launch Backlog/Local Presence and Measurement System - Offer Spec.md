# Local Presence & Measurement System

## Customer problem

A local business may have a Google Business Profile, website, reviews and customer questions, but no reliable operating rhythm for keeping information accurate, responding consistently, measuring local discovery or turning activity into decisions.

## Outcome

A maintained, human-approved system that helps the business:

- keep its Google Business Profile accurate;
- understand how customers discover and contact it;
- respond to reviews and questions in an authentic voice;
- connect local discovery with website/conversion evidence;
- identify practical actions for the next month.

No ranking or revenue guarantee is made.

## Service ladder

### 1. Local Presence Audit

One-off audit covering:

- business information and hours;
- categories and attributes;
- photos/media freshness;
- review-response gaps;
- unanswered customer questions;
- action links and website path;
- profile performance metrics;
- initial website/GA4 measurement check where access exists.

Deliverable: prioritised audit and 30-day action list.

### 2. Profile Repair and Context Pack

Implement approved corrections and create the business context layer:

- services and service descriptions;
- FAQs;
- tone/brand voice examples;
- response rules and escalation triggers;
- seasonal-hours process;
- approved post themes;
- owner/manager access map;
- change log.

### 3. Monthly Local Intelligence Brief

Monthly report in plain English:

- what changed;
- how people found the business;
- calls, directions, website clicks, bookings and relevant GA4 conversions;
- review and Q&A themes;
- profile gaps;
- recommended actions;
- unresolved measurement limitations.

### 4. Review and Q&A Support

A human-reviewed workflow for:

- drafting responses to new reviews;
- escalating complaints or sensitive issues;
- answering legitimate profile questions;
- identifying recurring customer questions for the website and FAQs.

No automatic posting without explicit client approval.

## Access and privacy rules

- Client retains Business Profile ownership.
- We receive Manager access where needed; no passwords are requested.
- Consent and approved scope are recorded in writing.
- Google data is clearly separated from GA4, Search Console and other data.
- Personal customer information is minimised in reports.
- Sensitive reviews/complaints are escalated for client approval.
- Client can end access and service cleanly.

## Qualification questions

- Do you serve customers at a physical location or service area?
- Do you own/manage one verified Business Profile?
- Who currently controls the profile?
- Are reviews or customer questions going unanswered?
- Is GA4 installed and trustworthy enough to use?
- What customer action matters most: call, booking, quote request, visit or purchase?
