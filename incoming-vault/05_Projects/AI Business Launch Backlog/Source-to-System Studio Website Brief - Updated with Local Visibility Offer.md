# Website brief

## Positioning

Source-to-System Studio turns scattered business knowledge, customer signals and operational data into clear, reusable systems and decisions.

## Primary offers

1. Business Context Brain
2. Local Visibility & Reputation Operations
3. Measurement & Growth Intelligence
4. Source-to-Revenue Sprint
5. Quote Intelligence and SOP Extraction

## Homepage flow

1. Clear outcome headline.
2. Three proof-oriented problems: scattered knowledge, neglected local presence, unclear customer signals.
3. Offer cards with who it is for, deliverable and starting conversation.
4. Before/after workflow diagram.
5. Privacy/access-control statement.
6. Sanitised demonstration or pilot proof.
7. Process: discover → audit → map → implement → review.
8. Contact/fit-check CTA.

## Analytics/local visibility page

Explain:

- Google Business Profile completeness and response operations.
- Review/Q&A drafting with human approval.
- Search/call/direction signals as indicators, not revenue proof.
- GA4 and Search Console measurement as an add-on.
- Client-owned accounts and named manager access.
- No guaranteed rankings or unsupported growth claims.

## Launch stack

Static site, custom domain, Cloudflare Pages, Google Workspace contact path, optional booking link. No client portal or automated public posting in version one.
