# Local Visibility & Reputation Operations

## Plain-English promise

Keep your Google Business Profile accurate, useful and responsive, while turning its signals into clear monthly actions.

## Best-fit clients

- Local trades and home-service businesses
- Health and allied-health practices, subject to privacy boundaries
- Cafes, hospitality and retail
- Property and local professional services
- Small operators who receive reviews and enquiries but do not maintain their profile consistently

## Service tiers

### Profile Clarity Audit

One-time audit covering:

- verification and access status;
- business name/category/contact details;
- hours and special-hours process;
- services/products/attributes;
- photos and recency;
- reviews and unanswered Q&A;
- calls, direction requests and search visibility where available;
- priority fixes and evidence required.

Deliverable: a short action plan, not a vague SEO report.

### Profile Reset Sprint

Audit plus human-reviewed implementation of approved changes:

- profile information;
- services and products;
- photos/assets supplied by the client;
- approved posts;
- review-response backlog;
- Q&A response backlog;
- brand-voice response guide.

### Local Visibility Maintenance

Monthly or fortnightly service:

- new-review response drafts;
- Q&A monitoring;
- hours/holiday-hours checks;
- profile-change checks;
- approved post suggestions;
- performance snapshot;
- next-action report.

### Measurement & Growth Intelligence add-on

Separate measurement layer:

- GA4 property/tag audit;
- key events/conversion definitions;
- Search Console and Business Profile signal review;
- enquiry-source tracking;
- simple dashboard or monthly decision brief;
- recommendations tied to business goals.

## Controls

- Client remains the Business Profile owner.
- Access uses named Google manager permissions, never password sharing.
- No public change is made without client approval unless a written standing authority exists.
- Sensitive industries require privacy review before handling review content or analytics data.
- Review replies must not reveal confidential customer information or make unsupported claims.
- No guaranteed rankings, traffic or revenue.

## Proof required before selling aggressively

- Sanitised demonstration audit.
- One local-business pilot.
- Before/after profile completeness evidence.
- Time-to-deliver measurement.
- Client-approved sample review responses.
- Clear distinction between visibility metrics and actual enquiries/sales.
