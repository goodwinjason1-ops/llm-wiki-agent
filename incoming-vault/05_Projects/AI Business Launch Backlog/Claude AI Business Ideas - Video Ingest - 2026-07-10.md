## What fits our current AI business section best

### Best immediate fit — Content Distribution / Source-to-System service

The video's content distribution idea maps almost perfectly onto what Ari already does for Jayse:

```text
YouTube / podcast / webinar / docs
→ transcript/source ingest
→ summary + snippets
→ lead magnet / offer page / email sequence / dashboard
→ reusable client asset
```

This is basically the **Video/Expert Content → Digital Product Factory** and **Business Context Brain** lanes we already identified.

A sharper version for Jayse:

> “I turn your long-form video, podcast, workshop, or messy business material into a reusable client decision kit, lead magnet, content engine, and AI-readable business brain.”

This avoids being just another generic content repurposing agency.

### Second-best fit — GEO/AEO visibility audit as add-on

The AI SEO idea is interesting, but we should avoid promising rankings. Safer offer:

> “AI Visibility Snapshot: what ChatGPT/Claude/Google-style answers currently say about your category, brand, competitors, and buyer questions — plus the content assets you need to improve your presence.”

This could bolt onto Business Context Brain:

- ingest client docs/site/content;
- identify missing proof/assets;
- create FAQ/answer pages;
- create blog/lead magnet topics;
- track whether AI answers mention the brand/category over time.

### Third-best fit — Ghostwriting for a vertical, not generic LinkedIn

Generic AI ghostwriting is crowded. A sharper wedge:

> “Founder Proof Posts for local experts: turn your real projects, quotes, case notes, and client wins into credible LinkedIn/newsletter content.”

Best verticals for Jayse's current systems:

- renovators/builders/tradies with project photos/quotes;
- buyer agents/property service providers;
- coaches/sports program operators;
- AOD/community-service orgs only if ethically appropriate and non-sensitive.
