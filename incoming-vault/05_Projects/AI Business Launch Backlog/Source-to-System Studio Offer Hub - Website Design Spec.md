# Website design spec

## Positioning

**Turn scattered business knowledge, customer signals and operational data into systems your team can actually use.**

Source-to-System Studio helps small businesses turn documents, reviews, website activity and repeated questions into clearer decisions, repeatable workflows and useful AI context.

## Primary audience

- Local service businesses
- Trades and home-service operators
- Consultants and agencies
- Small professional-service teams
- Businesses with neglected profiles, scattered documents or repetitive customer questions

## Site map

1. Home
2. Offers
3. Local Presence & Measurement System
4. Business Context Brain
5. Source-to-Revenue Sprint
6. Proof / How it works
7. About
8. Contact
9. Privacy and third-party disclosure

## Homepage sections

### Hero

**Make your business easier to find, understand and run.**

We turn scattered business knowledge, customer feedback and operational data into practical systems, decision briefs and repeatable workflows.

CTA: **Request a fit check**
Secondary CTA: **See the offers**

### Problem

- Important knowledge is spread across folders, inboxes, documents and conversations.
- Google profiles and websites are often not maintained consistently.
- Customer questions and reviews reveal useful information but nobody has time to analyse it.
- Teams repeatedly explain the same things.

### Offer cards

- Business Context Brain
- Local Presence & Measurement System
- Source-to-Revenue Sprint
- Quote and SOP Intelligence

### Method

```text
Discover → Inventory → Extract → Map → Improve → Maintain
```

### Proof

Use anonymised before/after examples and clearly label them as demonstrations until paid client results exist.

### Trust strip

- Human-reviewed
- Owner-controlled access
- No ranking guarantees
- No passwords requested
- Clear change log
- Privacy-aware delivery

### CTA

> Tell us what is scattered, repetitive or difficult to measure. We’ll suggest the smallest useful first step.

## Offer page: Local Presence & Measurement System

Include:

- customer problem;
- what is included;
- what is not included;
- audit/repair/monthly options;
- Business Profile and GA4 distinction;
- owner access and consent rules;
- indicative pilot range;
- example deliverables;
- CTA.

## Contact form

Fields:

- name;
- business;
- email;
- website/Google Business Profile link;
- location/service area;
- what feels scattered or underperforming;
- primary goal: calls / bookings / quote requests / visits / workflow clarity;
- approximate document/data volume;
- consent to receive a reply.

No sensitive customer data should be submitted through the initial form.

## Footer disclosures

- Source-to-System Studio is independent and not Google.
- Google Business Profile is a Google service provided at no extra charge; our fee is for support, analysis and approved management work.
- No guaranteed rankings, traffic or revenue.
- Client retains profile ownership and can end access.
- Link to Google's third-party disclosure notice.
- Privacy and cookie/analytics information.

## Build recommendation

Start as a static, mobile-first site on Cloudflare Pages with a custom domain. Use a Google Workspace form or simple approved enquiry route. Add booking only after the first copy and qualification flow is tested.

Do not build a client portal, payment flow, chatbot or automated profile-management system in v1.
