# Basketball Coach Game Plan Proof Pack - 2026-07-10

## Bottom-line offer

**I turn your team roster, recent game notes, and coaching goals into a clear game-plan pack you can use at training and on game day.**

Built as a service-first proof artifact before rebuilding or monetizing the full Basketball PWA.

## Why this is the first experiment

The Basketball PWA already has a deep feature base: training plans, drill library, match assessment, rotation tracker, coaching board, film room, development analytics, stats, parent/player portals, and admin reports.

Rather than rebuilding the app blindly, this proof pack tests the highest-value buyer question first:

> Will local coaches pay for a clearer plan that saves prep time and improves game-day decisions?

## Target buyer

Primary:

- junior/local basketball coaches,
- team managers who support coaches,
- club development coordinators.

Secondary:

- parents helping with team admin,
- rep/select team staff,
- private skill-development coaches.

## MVP deliverable

A polished PDF/Notion/Markdown-style pack with:

1. **Team snapshot** — roster, roles, strengths, development needs.
2. **Opponent / game context** — if available.
3. **Training focus for the week** — 2–3 priorities.
4. **Drill plan** — warm-up, skill block, team concept, game simulation.
5. **Rotation / lineup notes** — simple fair-minutes and role-aware rotation guidance.
6. **Game-day checklist** — coach reminders, bench cues, timeout prompts.
7. **Stats capture sheet** — basic manual tally template.
8. **Post-game review template** — what worked, what to adjust next week.

## Inputs needed from a coach

Minimum viable intake:

```text
Team age/group:
Number of players:
Training day/time:
Next game/opponent:
Top 3 team strengths:
Top 3 issues to fix:
Any players needing focus:
Current drills/plays used:
What would make this week easier for you as coach?
```

Optional:

- roster list,
- last game score/stats,
- short video clips,
- existing play names,
- coach notes,
- club development goals.

## Pricing test

| Package | Price test | Deliverable | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free sample | $0 | 1-page mini plan from fake/demo data | Gets feedback fast |
| Pilot pack | $49–$99 AUD | Full one-week coach game-plan pack | Tests first-cash willingness |
| Team month | $199–$399 AUD | 4 weekly packs + templates | Tests subscription appetite |
| Club trial | $499–$999 AUD | 3 teams / 1 month / coordinator summary | Tests B2B path |

## Validation target

Within 14 days:

- show demo to 3–5 coaches,
- get at least 2 coaches to share real/fake team details,
- get 1 paid pilot or strong “I would pay if…” objection,
- identify the top paid wedge: game plan, stats capture, drills, rotations, or full app.

## Success criteria

Proceed toward paid product/PWA if:

- coaches say it saves real prep time,
- at least one coach wants a second pack,
- the output creates obvious screenshots/demo moments,
- the workflow can be completed in under 60–90 minutes manually,
- repeated sections can be templated.

Pause/revise if:

- coaches only want free templates,
- the pack is too generic,
- input collection is too painful,
- no one cares unless it is fully integrated into their existing system.

## Productization path

1. Manual proof pack.
2. Repeatable template library.
3. Lightweight PWA landing/demo.
4. Basketball PWA feature packaging:
   - Coach board,
   - training plan builder,
   - rotation tracker,
   - stats capture,
   - player development analytics.
5. Subscription or club package.
