# Basketball Coach Game Plan Outreach Scripts

## DM / SMS — warm coach contact

Hey [Name] — quick one. I’m testing a basketball coaching helper that turns a few team notes into a practical one-week game-plan pack: training focus, drills, rotation notes, game-day checklist, and simple stats sheet.

I’m not selling a big app yet — I’m trying to see if this actually saves coaches time.

Would you be open to me making a free 1-page sample for your team from basic/fake/anonymized details?

## DM / SMS — parent/team manager

Hey [Name], I’m testing a simple tool for junior basketball teams: a weekly coach pack that helps with training focus, rotations, game-day checklist, and post-game review.

Do you know a coach/team manager who might be willing to look at a free sample and tell me if it would be useful?

## Short email

Subject: Free sample game-plan pack for your basketball team?

Hi [Name],

I’m testing a new basketball coaching workflow and looking for feedback from local coaches.

The idea is simple: you send a few team details — age group, roster size, next opponent, strengths, and what needs work — and I turn it into a practical one-week game-plan pack.

It includes:

- training focus for the week,
- drill plan,
- rotation/lineup notes,
- game-day checklist,
- simple stats capture sheet,
- post-game review template.

I’m not asking you to adopt a new app. I’m just trying to see if this saves coaches time and what parts are actually useful.

Would you be open to a free 1-page sample using fake or anonymized team details?

Thanks,
Jayse

## Follow-up after sample

Hey [Name], thanks again for looking at the sample.

Three quick questions:

1. Which section would you actually use at training or game day?
2. What feels missing or too generic?
3. If this was done for your team each week, would it be worth paying for — or would it need to be part of a bigger app/dashboard?

No pressure — blunt feedback is more useful than polite feedback.

## Feedback questions for call/chat

- What takes the most time each week as coach?
- How do you currently plan training?
- Do you track stats or player development?
- Are rotations/fair minutes a pain point?
- Would parents/players need access, or just the coach?
- Would you prefer PDF, mobile page, spreadsheet, or app dashboard?
- What would make this worth $49–$99 for a pilot?
- What would make it worth a monthly subscription?

## Offer close if interested

If you want, I can do one full pilot pack for your team next week. I’m testing $49–$99 AUD for the first version while I refine it. If it doesn’t save you time or give you anything useful, I’d rather know that now than build the wrong thing.
