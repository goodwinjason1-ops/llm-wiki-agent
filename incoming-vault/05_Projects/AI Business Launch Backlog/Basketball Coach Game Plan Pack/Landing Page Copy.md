## Hero

A practical coaching pack built from your roster, recent game notes, and training goals — so you can walk into training and game day with a plan, not a scramble.

CTA: **Get a sample coach pack**

## Problem

Coaching a junior/local team means juggling:

- training plans,
- player development,
- rotations,
- parent communication,
- game-day decisions,
- stats and post-game notes.

Most coaches do not need another complicated app first. They need a simple, useful plan for the next training session and game.

## Offer

Send through a few team details and receive a practical game-plan pack with:

- training focus for the week,
- drill plan,
- lineup/rotation notes,
- game-day checklist,
- simple stats capture sheet,
- post-game review template.

## Who it is for

- junior basketball coaches,
- team managers supporting coaches,
- club development coordinators,
- parents helping run team admin.

## How it works

1. **Send team context** — age group, roster size, next game, strengths, issues to fix.
2. **Get a draft plan** — a clear pack you can review before training.
3. **Use it on court** — print it, keep it on your phone, or turn it into your team workflow.
4. **Give feedback** — help shape the future coaching app / team dashboard.

## Pilot pricing test

## Trust / guardrail copy

This is a coaching organization tool, not a promise of wins or player selection outcomes. Youth/player data can be anonymized. The pilot is designed to test what coaches actually find useful before building more software.
