# Source-to-Revenue Sprint - Outreach Messages

## Target buyer examples

- renovators/builders/tradies with project photos, quotes, and FAQs;
- buyer agents/property advisers with checklists and buyer questions;
- local sports coaches/program operators;
- consultants/educators with webinars or podcasts;
- service businesses with messy Google Drive folders and no lead magnet.

## DM version — soft teardown

Hi [Name], I noticed you’ve got strong [projects/content/expertise] around [specific thing].

I’m testing a small service where I turn one existing video, folder, webinar, or project proof set into a practical lead magnet + 5–10 ready-to-use posts/email snippets.

No generic AI spam — the idea is to package what you already know into something clients can actually understand and act on.

Would you be open to me doing a quick free mini-teardown of one piece of your existing content/material and showing what assets could be pulled from it?

## Email version

Subject: quick idea for repurposing your existing [content/projects]

Hi [Name],

I came across your [website/video/project/page] and noticed you already have a lot of useful knowledge around [specific topic].

I’m validating a small “source-to-revenue” service where I take an existing video, webinar, project folder, quote set, or messy business material and turn it into:

- a simple lead magnet idea,
- 5–10 post/email drafts,
- a source map of the strongest buyer questions,
- and a practical next-asset plan.

Would you be open to a free mini-teardown? I’d use one public or shared source from your business and send back a short before/after example.

No obligation — I’m mainly looking for feedback on whether this is useful for operators like you.

Regards,
Jason

## Follow-up

Hi [Name], just circling back on this.

The quick version: if you send one video, PDF, project page, or folder, I’ll show how I’d turn it into a lead magnet + a few useful posts/emails.

If it’s not useful, no worries — the feedback still helps me sharpen the offer.

## Qualification questions

1. What source material do you already have?
2. What buyer/client question comes up most often?
3. Do you currently have a lead magnet or email sequence?
4. Would you prefer more leads, better trust-building content, or a cleaner internal knowledge base?
5. If the mini teardown is useful, would a done-for-you Proof Pack at AUD $297–$497 be worth considering?
