## Strategic conclusion

These videos strengthen the case for **Business Context Brain** more than they change the immediate BuyerProof plan.

The strongest combined offer is:

> A service that turns messy company files, videos, quotes, brainstorms, and project docs into a persistent AI-readable business context vault.

This also becomes our internal proof: Jayse's own projects are the case study.

## Product implications

- Add "video/source ingestion" as a visible feature.
- Use the phrase: **your AI should not reset every time a chat resets**.
- Package the first offer as a service sprint, not SaaS.
- Use Reddit/community scanning later to find niches complaining about documentation chaos, onboarding mess, quoting inconsistency, and lost SOPs.
