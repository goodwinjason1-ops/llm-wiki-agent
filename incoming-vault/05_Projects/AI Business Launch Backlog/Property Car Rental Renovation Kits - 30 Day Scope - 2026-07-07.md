# Property, Used-Car, Rental and Renovation Kits - 30 Day Scope

## Executive summary

The strongest cluster is **Australian high-stakes consumer decision kits**:

1. Property buyer due diligence kit.
2. Used-car buyer inspection kit.
3. Rental/bond-clean exit kit.
4. Renovation quote/spec comparison kits for high-ticket home improvements.

These are better than the AI receptionist idea because they are:

- consumer-facing or partner-distribution friendly,
- high anxiety / high consequence,
- template/checklist/calculator friendly,
- fast to produce as MVPs,
- scalable into bundles, directories, affiliate/referral partnerships, and local service lead-gen.

Important: these products must be framed as **educational checklists and preparation tools**, not legal, financial, building, mechanical, or tenancy advice.

## Opportunity ranking

| Rank | Product | Buyer pain | 30-day launch | Monetization | Scalability | Overall |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Australian Property Due Diligence Kit** | Huge purchase, fear of missing defects/legal issues | Medium-fast | High | High | Best strategic asset |
| 2 | **Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit** | Fear of buying lemon/finance write-off | Fast | Medium | High | Best quick MVP |
| 3 | **Rental Exit / Bond-Back Kit** | Renters fear losing bond; cleaners need leads | Fast | Medium | Medium-high | Great companion product |
| 4 | **Renovation Quote Comparison Kit** | High-ticket quotes hard to compare | Medium-fast | High | High | Strong B2B + consumer path |
| 5 | **Bathroom/Kitchen Renovation Readiness Kit** | Expensive, confusing, quote-heavy | Medium | High | High | Best home-reno niche wedge |

## Product 1 — Australian Property Due Diligence Kit

### Product concept

A practical Australian buyer pack that helps first-home buyers and investors prepare before making an offer or going unconditional.

Working names:

- Property Buyer Red Flag Kit Australia
- Australian Property Due Diligence Pack
- Before You Buy: Australian Property Checklist
- Home Buyer Risk Checklist AU

### Core buyer

- First-home buyers.
- Rentvestors / first investment property buyers.
- Buyers purchasing interstate.
- Buyers without a buyer’s agent.
- People overwhelmed by contracts, building/pest, strata, zoning, flood/fire risk, and finance dates.

### MVP deliverables

1. **Inspection-day checklist**
   - exterior, roofline, drainage, cracks, moisture signs, windows/doors, plumbing/electrical visible issues, noise, parking, neighbours, sunlight, mobile reception.

2. **Due diligence tracker spreadsheet**
   - title/search, contract review, finance date, building & pest, strata/body corporate, council/zoning, easements/covenants, flood/bushfire, insurance quote, rental estimate, comparable sales.

3. **Offer-readiness checklist**
   - finance pre-approval, solicitor/conveyancer, building/pest booking, deposit readiness, key dates, cooling-off/auction notes by state.

4. **Agent question scripts**
   - “Have there been insurance claims?”
   - “Are there unapproved works?”
   - “Any easements or shared access?”
   - “Why is the vendor selling?”
   - “Any strata special levies?”

5. **Risk scoring sheet**
   - green/yellow/red risk matrix.
   - Not a recommendation engine; just a decision aid.

6. **Professional handoff list**
   - building inspector, pest inspector, conveyancer/solicitor, mortgage broker, insurance, strata search provider.

### Pricing

| Version | Price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Lite PDF checklist | Free / A$9 | Lead magnet or tripwire |
| Full spreadsheet + scripts | A$29–A$49 | First paid product |
| Premium buyer pack | A$79–A$149 | Includes Notion/Sheets dashboard + state notes |
| Concierge setup / review prep | A$250–A$750 | Help buyer organize documents/questions; no legal advice |

### Distribution options

- Direct-to-consumer via YouTube/TikTok/SEO.
- Partnerships with buyer’s agents, mortgage brokers, conveyancers, building inspectors.
- Affiliate/referral relationships with inspection/search providers where compliant.
- Local property Facebook groups.
- Reddit/community posts only if value-first and non-spammy.

### 30-day implementation

#### Week 1 — MVP build

- Build checklist spreadsheet and PDF.
- Include disclaimers.
- Make one sample property walkthrough using a fictional listing.
- Create landing page and checkout/waitlist.

Deliverable: **v0 Property Buyer Red Flag Kit**.

#### Week 2 — Expert review and landing page

- Ask 2–3 relevant professionals for feedback: conveyancer, broker, building inspector, buyer’s agent.
- Update language to avoid legal/financial advice.
- Create 3 demo posts/videos:
  - “7 red flags before buying a house in Australia.”
  - “What to ask before making an offer.”
  - “Building and pest is not the only check.”

Deliverable: reviewed v1 + marketing assets.

#### Week 3 — Outreach and partner tests

- Contact 30 buyer’s agents/brokers/inspectors/conveyancers.
- Offer co-branded lead magnet version.
- Post in 3–5 property communities with useful free checklist.
- Test price A$29/A$49.

Deliverable: first downloads/sales/partner conversations.

#### Week 4 — Bundle and refine

- Add state-specific tabs/notes.
- Add inspection photo log.
- Add email course: “5 days to offer-ready.”
- Build v2 premium bundle.

Success checkpoint:

- 5+ sales or 3+ partner conversations or 50+ free downloads.

## Product 2 — Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit Australia

### Product concept

A buyer checklist and decision pack for Australians buying a used car privately or from a dealer.

Working names:

- Don’t Buy a Lemon: AU Used Car Buyer Kit
- Used Car Red Flag Kit Australia
- PPSR + Inspection Checklist Pack
- Private Car Buyer Safety Kit AU

### Why it is attractive

- Faster MVP than property.
- Clear pain: fear of buying a lemon, written-off vehicle, finance owing, odometer issues, hidden damage.
- Government sources already educate around registration, condition, test drive, PPSR, service history, odometer, and pre-purchase inspection.
- Easy to create video/short-form content.

### MVP deliverables

1. **Inspection checklist**
   - exterior, tyres, lights, fluids, leaks, interior, electronics, service history, VIN/rego, warning lights.

2. **Test-drive checklist**
   - cold start, braking, steering, gearbox, suspension noises, temperature, acceleration, reverse, parking sensors/camera.

3. **PPSR/VIN/rego tracker**
   - write-off, stolen, finance owing, VIN match, registration status.

4. **Seller message scripts**
   - request VIN, service history, reason for selling, permission for inspection.

5. **Negotiation calculator**
   - asking price, market comps, repairs, tyres, rego, service due, inspection findings.

6. **Mechanic handoff sheet**
   - what to ask a pre-purchase inspector.

### Pricing

| Version | Price |
|---|---:|
| Free red-flag checklist | Free / A$5 |
| Full buyer spreadsheet + scripts | A$19–A$39 |
| Premium car-buying bundle | A$49–A$99 |
| Concierge comparison setup | A$99–A$250 |

### Distribution

- TikTok/YouTube Shorts: “Don’t buy until you check this.”
- Partnerships with mobile mechanics, detailers, pre-purchase inspection services.
- Facebook Marketplace car-buying groups, value-first.
- SEO pages for “used car checklist Australia”, “PPSR checklist”, “private car buyer checklist”.

### 30-day implementation

#### Week 1

- Build PDF + Google Sheet.
- Include PPSR/VIN/rego checklist and disclaimers.
- Create fictional car example.

#### Week 2

- Get feedback from mechanic/detailer/car enthusiast.
- Build landing page + checkout.
- Create 10 short-form scripts.

#### Week 3

- Contact 30 mobile mechanics/detailers/car inspection businesses.
- Offer co-branded lead magnet.
- Post free checklist to relevant communities.

#### Week 4

- Add comparison calculator.
- Add “private seller message pack.”
- Bundle into v2.

Success checkpoint:

- 10+ sales/downloads or 2+ partner opportunities.

## Product 3 — Rental Exit / Bond-Back Kit Australia

### Product concept

A renter checklist, photo-proof tracker, and cleaner handoff pack to help tenants prepare for end-of-lease inspections and protect their bond.

Working names:

- Bond Back Checklist Australia
- Rental Exit Proof Pack
- End-of-Lease Inspection Kit
- Move-Out Clean Tracker AU

### Why it is attractive

- Clear pain: renters fear losing bond.
- Cleaning businesses can use it as a lead magnet.
- Pairs naturally with property due diligence and moving-house content.
- Easy MVP.

### MVP deliverables

1. **Room-by-room cleaning checklist**
   - kitchen, oven, bathroom, laundry, bedrooms, windows/tracks, walls, floors, outdoor areas.

2. **Photo-proof tracker**
   - before/after photos by room.
   - damage vs cleaning notes.

3. **Agent/property-manager email scripts**
   - inspection booking, issue clarification, repair/cleaning confirmation.

4. **Cleaner quote request template**
   - property size, bathrooms, carpets, oven, balcony, pet/flea treatment, keys/access.

5. **Final inspection readiness score**
   - simple green/yellow/red.

### Pricing

| Version | Price |
|---|---:|
| Free checklist | Free |
| Full renter pack | A$9–A$19 |
| Premium moving bundle | A$29–A$49 |
| Co-branded cleaner lead magnet | A$250–A$750 setup for cleaning businesses |

### Distribution

- Renters groups.
- Student housing groups.
- Moving/removalist partnerships.
- End-of-lease cleaners.
- Property managers as a “reduce disputes” handout.

### 30-day implementation

#### Week 1

- Build checklist PDF + photo tracker.
- Create moving timeline.
- Build simple landing page.

#### Week 2

- Get cleaner/property-manager feedback.
- Create co-branded version for cleaners.
- Create 10 short posts/videos.

#### Week 3

- Contact 30 cleaners/removalists/property managers.
- Offer lead magnet setup.
- Test free download → paid premium pack.

#### Week 4

- Bundle with property buyer/renter moving kit.
- Add email reminders and printable room labels.

Success checkpoint:

- 50+ free downloads or 1 cleaner/removalist lead magnet client.

## Product 4 — Renovation Quote Comparison Kit

### Product concept

A homeowner toolkit for comparing renovation quotes and avoiding vague/incomplete scopes.

This sits near bathrooms but can expand into many high-ticket renovation categories.

Working names:

- Renovation Quote Decoder Australia
- Homeowner Quote Comparison Kit
- Reno Scope & Quote Checker
- Before You Sign: Renovation Quote Pack

### Why it is attractive

- High-ticket decisions.
- Consumers struggle to compare quotes apples-to-apples.
- Existing Australian consumer guidance has many building/renovation checklist categories: home improvements under/over $10k, contracts, permits, payments, during construction, completion.
- Can serve both consumers and good contractors who want better-educated leads.

### MVP deliverables

1. **Quote comparison spreadsheet**
   - scope, exclusions, allowances, materials, labour, timeline, warranty, insurance, licence, payment stages.

2. **Scope clarification email scripts**
   - “Can you confirm whether waterproofing/permits/rubbish removal are included?”

3. **Red flag checklist**
   - vague allowances, huge deposits, missing licence/insurance, unclear variations, no timeline, no exclusions.

4. **Project readiness checklist**
   - photos, measurements, inspiration, must-haves, budget range, timeline, access constraints.

5. **Contract/pre-signing question list**
   - educational only; lawyer/building professional review recommended.

### Pricing

| Version | Price |
|---|---:|
| Free quote red flags checklist | Free |
| Full quote comparison kit | A$29–A$79 |
| Premium renovation planning pack | A$99–A$199 |
| Contractor-branded version | A$500–A$1,500 setup |

### First high-ticket subniches

| Rank | Niche | Why |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Kitchens | Expensive, many components, appliance/joinery choices |
| 2 | Bathrooms | Waterproofing, fixtures, tile choices, high fear of defects |
| 3 | Pools/spas | Very high ticket, compliance/maintenance questions |
| 4 | Solar/battery | Quote complexity, rebates, warranties, hardware comparisons |
| 5 | Roofing/restoration | Urgent/high-value, insurance/storm concerns |
| 6 | Decks/pergolas/outdoor rooms | Permits/materials/timber/compliance choices |
| 7 | Flooring | Materials, prep, install differences, measurable scope |
| 8 | Windows/doors/double glazing | Energy, noise, security, high unit costs |
| 9 | Sheds/granny flats/studios | Permits, site prep, council rules, big-ticket |
| 10 | Landscaping/retaining walls | Drainage, materials, earthworks, visual proof |

### 30-day implementation

#### Week 1

- Pick one subniche: kitchens, bathrooms, solar/battery, pools, or roofing.
- Build quote comparison sheet and red-flag checklist.
- Build sample quote comparison using fictional quotes.

#### Week 2

- Get feedback from 2 contractors or homeowners.
- Create landing page and free lead magnet.
- Create 5 short videos/posts.

#### Week 3

- Contact 30 contractors and 10 homeowner/community groups.
- Offer contractor-branded lead magnet.
- Test paid consumer pack.

#### Week 4

- Add subniche-specific tabs.
- Create premium bundle.
- Package contractor setup service.

Success checkpoint:

- 1 contractor-branded lead magnet client or 10+ consumer purchases/downloads.

## Best combined strategy

Rather than launching four separate products, build one umbrella brand:

# BuyerProof AU

Positioning:

> “Practical Australian checklists and decision kits for expensive life admin: buying property, buying cars, moving rentals, and renovating.”

Initial product ladder:

1. Free checklist downloads.
2. A$19–A$49 paid kits.
3. A$99–A$199 premium bundles.
4. A$500–A$1,500 co-branded lead magnets for service providers.
5. Later: directories, affiliate/referral partnerships, and calculators.

## Recommended order of execution

### Fastest MVP

Start with **Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit**.

Why:

- Simple to understand.
- Fast to make.
- Clear government-backed checks like PPSR, rego, odometer, service history, pre-purchase inspection.
- Easy short-form content.
- Can validate direct sales quickly.

### Best long-term strategic asset

Build **Australian Property Due Diligence Kit** next.

Why:

- High stakes.
- Search demand likely durable.
- Can partner with brokers, inspectors, buyer’s agents.
- Fits Australia’s busy property market.

### Best companion product

Add **Rental Exit / Bond-Back Kit**.

Why:

- Related to moving/property.
- Easier consumer purchase.
- Good lead magnet for cleaners/removalists/property managers.

### Best high-ticket B2B product

Build **Renovation Quote Comparison Kit** as the service-provider monetization path.

Why:

- Contractors can pay for branded versions.
- Homeowners need clarity.
- Expands from bathroom/kitchen into solar, roofing, pools, flooring, decks, landscaping, windows.

Strong success:

## Key legal/safety guardrails

- Add clear disclaimers: educational only; not legal, financial, building, mechanical, tenancy, or investment advice.
- Encourage professional inspections and advice.
- Do not give state-specific legal claims unless verified.
- Avoid “guarantee your bond back”, “guarantee no defects”, “guarantee safe purchase.”
- For property: recommend conveyancer/solicitor, building and pest inspector, insurance checks.
- For used cars: recommend PPSR, registration checks, pre-purchase mechanical inspection.
- For renovation: recommend licensed builders/trades, written contracts, local authority requirements.

## Final recommendation

Build **BuyerProof AU** as the umbrella.

First month product sequence:

1. Used-Car Buyer Inspection Kit.
2. Property Due Diligence Kit.
3. Rental Exit / Bond-Back Kit.
4. Renovation Quote Comparison Kit.

This creates a connected family of practical Australian decision tools for expensive consumer moments. It can generate small direct product sales quickly while also opening higher-value B2B opportunities with mechanics, cleaners, buyer’s agents, brokers, inspectors, renovators, and contractors.
