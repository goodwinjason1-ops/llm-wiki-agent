# AI Agent Income Ideas - Cap 1 2026-07-08

## Best opportunities for Jayse

### 1. Business Context Brain “Roast Report”

**Offer:** A paid decision kit that stress-tests a business idea using contrarian, expansionist, first-principles, market-research, and buyer-persona lenses.

**Deliverable:**
- green / reshape / kill verdict;
- fatal flaws;
- upside case;
- competitor/pricing snapshot;
- buyer objections;
- cheapest 48-hour validation test.

**Why it fits:** It turns the vague “AI business partner” idea into a clear, sellable document. It also protects Jayse from building generic ideas without demand.

### 2. Verified AI Landing Page + Waitlist Pack

**Offer:** Build a one-page waitlist/offer page and include verification evidence.

**Proof bundle:**
- desktop/mobile screenshots;
- button/link checks;
- form validation tests;
- edge-case submissions;
- bug list and fix notes.

**Why it fits:** BuyerProof AU is about proof and trust. Most AI-builders can produce a page; fewer deliver proof it actually works.

### 3. Agentic Launch Kit

**Offer:** Generate a launch kit for a focused niche offer.

**Files:**
- positioning;
- competitor research;
- 14-day launch plan;
- outreach templates;
- 25 personalized drafts;
- content calendar;
- handoff note.

**Why it fits:** This maps directly onto [[Business Launch Asset Navigation Dashboard]] and can be reused for clients.

### 4. AI Workflow Audit for Small Businesses

**Offer:** Audit how a business uses AI and install a lightweight SOP: capture, handoff, verification, and launch execution.

**Buyer:** consultants, tradies with admin burden, creators, small agencies, local service businesses.

**Why it fits:** It is service-first and avoids betting on a SaaS before demand exists.

## Suggested next test

Run the **Roast Report** process on Jayse's own Business Context Brain offer:

```text
Goal: decide whether Business Context Brain should first be sold as a one-off audit, a recurring content/ops retainer, or a packaged decision-kit service.
Evidence required: competitor examples, buyer persona objections, pricing ranges, 48-hour validation test, and one recommended offer page angle.
```
