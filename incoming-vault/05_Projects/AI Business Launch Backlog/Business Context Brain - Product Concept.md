# Business Context Brain - Product Concept

## One-line idea

A done-for-you AI context brain for small businesses: drop messy documents, quotes, plans, SOPs, meeting notes, brainstorms, PDFs, and screenshots into a vault; the system maps context, links related items, extracts action-ready knowledge, and gives the business a searchable/visual operational brain.

## Working names

- Business Context Brain
- OpsBrain Vault
- Company Context Map
- Quote-to-Knowledge Vault
- AI Second Brain for Business

## Buyer pain

Businesses often have valuable knowledge trapped in:

- quote PDFs and supplier emails,
- project scopes,
- SOPs and checklists,
- screenshots and brainstorm notes,
- policy docs,
- client notes,
- scattered Google Drive/OneDrive folders,
- staff knowledge in heads,
- old proposals and templates.

The pain is not "AI". The pain is:

- nobody can find the right context quickly,
- new staff need repeated explanations,
- quotes/proposals are rebuilt from scratch,
- knowledge leaves when people leave,
- AI assistants underperform because they lack clean business context.

## First offer: Context Map Sprint

**A$750–A$2,500 setup** depending on document volume.

Deliver in 7–10 days:

1. Intake folder and file inventory.
2. Obsidian-style vault or Markdown knowledge base.
3. Source summaries for key docs.
4. Wiki pages for entities/processes/offers/suppliers/clients.
5. Graph-ready links between notes.
6. Business dashboard: "Start Here", key areas, open decisions.
7. Reusable templates: quote summary, project note, SOP note, meeting note, decision log.
8. AI-ready context pack for Claude/Codex/Hermes.

## MVP implementation

Use Obsidian/Markdown first, not SaaS.

1. Client provides 30–200 documents in a shared folder.
2. Inventory script classifies file types and candidate topics.
3. AI extracts source summaries, entities, processes, decisions, risks, and reusable templates.
4. Vault gets generated with links and dashboards.
5. Deliver a walkthrough video and "how to keep it alive" workflow.

This offer is the commercial version of the [[karpathy-llm-wiki]] pattern: raw client documents stay preserved, the agent creates and maintains a linked synthesis layer, and recurring reviews surface missed connections, stale assumptions, and reusable workflows.

The sales message should be stronger than "document chat":

> We turn your scattered business documents into a maintained AI-readable intelligence layer — a context map your team and AI tools can actually use.

## Differentiation

Not just document chat. The value is:

- source preservation,
- durable summaries,
- graph links,
- project dashboards,
- workflows,
- reusable Claude/Codex rules,
- scheduled maintenance,
- human-readable vault, not locked in a black-box chatbot.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Privacy/confidentiality | Local-first vault, redaction, no secrets, client approval. |
| Hallucinated summaries | Keep raw sources linked; mark confidence and TODO verify. |
| Over-custom build | Service-first, Markdown-first, no SaaS until repeated demand. |
| Client overwhelm | Deliver dashboard and 5–10 key workflows, not just a giant note dump. |
| Regulated sectors | Educational/admin support only; no legal/clinical/financial advice. |

## 30-day validation plan

### Week 1 — Build Jayse demo

- Finish project registry and dashboards.
- Create 3 example extraction packs: code repo, business idea folder, education/course folder.
- Record a walkthrough.

### Week 2 — Package offer

- Create landing page copy.
- Create before/after screenshots.
- Define 3 packages.
- Build sample vault template.

### Week 3 — Outreach

Target 20 businesses with obvious messy document/process pain.

Pitch:

> "I turn your scattered business docs, quotes, SOPs, and brainstorms into a searchable AI-ready knowledge vault your team can actually use. Want me to map 20 sample docs and show you the before/after?"

### Week 4 — Pilot

- Sell/perform 1 discounted pilot.
- Deliver dashboard + context map + templates.
- Use feedback to decide whether this becomes a service line.
