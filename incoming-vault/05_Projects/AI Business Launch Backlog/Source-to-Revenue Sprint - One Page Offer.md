# Source-to-Revenue Sprint

## Headline

Turn your existing content, project proof, and messy business knowledge into reusable sales assets in 7 days.

## Who it is for

For operators, consultants, builders, coaches, educators, and local experts who already have useful knowledge or proof, but it is scattered across videos, PDFs, quotes, photos, notes, or old documents.

## The problem

You already have the raw material for better marketing and sales:

- videos and webinars;
- client/project notes;
- quotes and proposals;
- screenshots/photos;
- FAQs and repeated buyer questions;
- old PDFs or Google Drive folders.

But it is not packaged into assets that bring trust, leads, or repeatable sales conversations.

## The 7-day outcome

I turn one source set into:

- a clear source map;
- one lead magnet outline or draft;
- 10 repurposed posts/emails/social snippets;
- a 5-email follow-up sequence;
- a simple AI-readable business context vault;
- a before/after walkthrough.

## What this is not

- Not guaranteed leads.
- Not fake AI content spam.
- Not a replacement for your expertise.
- Not a black-box ad agency.

It is a practical way to turn what you already know into assets people can understand and act on.
