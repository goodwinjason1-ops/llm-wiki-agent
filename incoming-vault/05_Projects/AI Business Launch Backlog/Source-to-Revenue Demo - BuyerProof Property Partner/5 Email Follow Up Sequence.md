## Email 2 — What to photograph

Subject: What to photograph during an inspection

A quick tip before your next inspection: take photos of anything you may need to ask about later.

Examples:

- cracks;
- water stains;
- drainage issues;
- switchboard;
- roof/gutters if visible;
- strata/common areas for units/townhouses.

The checklist includes prompts so you do not rely on memory after the inspection.

## Email 3 — Questions to ask before offer/auction

Subject: Questions worth asking before you commit

Before you make an offer or bid, consider which professionals you may need to speak with.

Depending on the property, that might include a broker, conveyancer/solicitor, building/pest inspector, buyer agent, or insurance adviser.

The goal is not to delay forever — it is to avoid avoidable surprises.
