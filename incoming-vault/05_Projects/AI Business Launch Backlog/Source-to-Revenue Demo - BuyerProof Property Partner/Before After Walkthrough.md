# Before/After Walkthrough

## Before

The source material existed, but it was scattered across:

- BuyerProof partner one-pager;
- Business Context Brain concept note;
- Source-to-Revenue offer drafts;
- outreach scripts.

A prospect would need to read multiple internal notes to understand the commercial offer.

## After

The same source material now becomes a client-facing demo pack:

1. source inventory;
2. source map;
3. buyer questions;
4. lead magnet outline;
5. proof posts;
6. email follow-up sequence;
7. walkthrough.

## What this proves

A Source-to-Revenue Sprint can turn existing knowledge into a practical revenue asset set without building software first.

## How to show it to a prospect

Say:

> “Here’s a small before/after. The raw material was a scattered set of notes about a property checklist. The output is a lead magnet outline, partner positioning, 5 posts, and a 5-email follow-up sequence. This is the same transformation I’d do with your video, webinar, project folder, or messy business docs.”
