# Buyer Questions

## End property buyer questions

1. What should I check before making an offer?
2. What red flags should I photograph during inspection?
3. What should I ask the agent?
4. When do I need a conveyancer, broker, building inspector, or buyer agent?
5. What should I verify locally/professionally before committing?

## Partner questions

1. Will this make me look useful before the buyer is ready to book?
2. Can I brand it without taking on advice risk?
3. Can it lead to calls/replies/bookings?
4. How fast can it be customised?
5. What do I have to provide?

## Source-to-Revenue proof questions

1. Can one existing note become a useful partner-facing asset?
2. Can we produce posts/emails from the same source?
3. Can this be sold as a done-for-you sprint?
4. Does the asset make sense to a non-AI-native operator?
