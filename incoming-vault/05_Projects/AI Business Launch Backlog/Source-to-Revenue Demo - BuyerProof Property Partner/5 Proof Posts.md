# 5 Proof Posts

## Post 1 — Broker angle

Most buyers think pre-approval is the only finance step before an inspection.

But inspection day is when buyers start making emotional decisions.

A simple checklist can help them slow down and ask better questions before they bid, offer, or go unconditional.

That is why I’m testing a co-branded Australian Property Inspection-Day Red Flag Checklist for brokers and buyer-support professionals.

Educational only — not financial/legal/property advice. Just a practical way to help buyers prepare.

## Post 2 — Buyer agent angle

A buyer agent’s best clients are usually the ones who understand what needs checking before they commit.

A practical inspection-day checklist can help buyers:

- photograph red flags;
- ask sharper questions;
- know when to call a professional;
- avoid rushing under pressure.

I’m testing a co-branded version for property professionals who want a useful lead magnet, not generic content.

## Post 3 — Conveyancer angle

A property checklist should not tell people what to buy.

It should remind them what to verify before they commit.

That means contract/title review, conditions, strata/body corporate issues, and professional handoff points stay front and centre.

I’m packaging this as a simple co-branded lead magnet for Australian property professionals.

## Post 4 — Source-to-Revenue demo angle

This is the kind of asset a Source-to-Revenue Sprint creates:

Raw business material → clear buyer questions → lead magnet → posts/emails → follow-up sequence.

The goal is not “AI content”.

The goal is turning what a business already knows into assets clients can use.
