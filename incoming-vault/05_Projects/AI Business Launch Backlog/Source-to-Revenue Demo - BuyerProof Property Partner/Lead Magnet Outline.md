# Lead Magnet Outline

## Title

**Australian Property Inspection-Day Red Flag Checklist**

## Subtitle

A practical checklist to help buyers slow down, document concerns, and know what to verify with qualified professionals before committing.

## Sections

1. Before you leave home
   - finance/pre-approval status;
   - suburb/property research;
   - inspection supplies;
   - questions to prepare.

2. At the inspection
   - visible condition red flags;
   - moisture/drainage signs;
   - strata/body corporate prompts;
   - renovation/extension questions;
   - photos to take.

3. Before making an offer or bidding
   - contract/title review reminder;
   - building/pest inspection reminder;
   - finance conditions reminder;
   - insurance/strata/cost checks.

4. Professional handoff
   - broker;
   - conveyancer/solicitor;
   - building/pest inspector;
   - buyer agent;
   - insurance adviser.

5. Partner CTA block
   - partner logo/contact;
   - booking link;
   - short disclaimer.

## Disclaimer

Educational checklist only. Not legal, financial, property, building, pest, insurance, tax, or investment advice. Verify locally and with qualified professionals.
