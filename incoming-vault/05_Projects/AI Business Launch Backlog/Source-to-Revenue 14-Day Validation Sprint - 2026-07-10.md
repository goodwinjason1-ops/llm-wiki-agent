# Source-to-Revenue 14-Day Validation Sprint

## Core offer

> I turn one long-form video, webinar, podcast, workshop, or messy business folder into a lead magnet, 10–20 repurposed posts, a 5-email follow-up sequence, and an AI-readable business context vault.

## Sprint goal

Validate whether non-AI-native operators want a packaged source-to-revenue transformation before building SaaS or a bigger agency.

## Best first demo options

1. **Business Context Brain demo** — strongest strategic fit.
2. **BuyerProof AU partner lead magnet** — strongest B2B/property wedge.
3. **Basketball Coach Game Plan Pack** — strongest personal-fit/sports wedge.

Recommendation: start with **Business Context Brain + BuyerProof AU partner pack** because the buyer pain is clearer and the deliverable is easy to show.

## Day-by-day plan

| Day | Action | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Choose demo source set | selected source folder + before/after angle |
| 2 | Build demo transformation | source map + lead magnet outline + 5 posts |
| 3 | Create one-page offer | offer page draft |
| 4 | Create mini-teardown script | message + Loom/checklist template |
| 5 | Build prospect list | 20 operators |
| 6 | Send first 10 messages | outreach log |
| 7 | Send next 10 messages | outreach log |
| 8 | Follow up interested replies | call notes |
| 9 | Deliver 1 free/low-cost mini teardown | before/after proof |
| 10 | Ask for paid Proof Pack | offer sent |
| 11 | Iterate positioning | objections log |
| 12 | Send follow-up batch | 10 more prospects if needed |
| 13 | Decide package/pricing | continue/pivot decision |
| 14 | Review evidence | validation report |

## Validation metrics

Continue if at least one occurs:

- 3+ positive replies from 20 targeted messages;
- 1 paid Proof Pack;
- 2+ people send source material for mini teardown;
- one clear vertical asks for the same deliverable.

Pause/pivot if:

- no replies after 30 targeted messages;
- people like the idea but will not send material;
- delivery takes too long to productize;
- buyers only want generic social posts with low willingness to pay.

## Pricing tests

| Package | Price test | Deliverables |
|---|---:|---|
| Proof Pack | AUD $297–$497 | 1 source → summary, 5 posts, lead magnet outline, source map |
| Sprint Pack | AUD $750–$1,500 | 1–3 sources → lead magnet, 10–20 posts, 5-email sequence, dashboard |
| Business Brain Add-on | AUD $1,500–$3,000 | source vault, intake templates, dashboard, monthly maintenance setup |

## Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed leads, rankings, or revenue.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, metrics, or client examples.
- Keep AI as the production advantage, not the whole promise.
- Use client-owned or public/permissioned source material only.
