# Business Context Brain — Local Prospect Shortlist
## Emerald anchor · approximate 50 km radius · 2026-07-13

## Research scope

This is a research shortlist, not an outreach list. Candidates were selected from public business websites, local directories and search results for Emerald, the Eastern/Dandenong Ranges, Ferntree Gully/Knox, Berwick and Pakenham. The 50 km boundary is treated as an approximate service-area screen from Emerald; confirm the exact business address and travel/service coverage before any contact.

The strongest fit is a business with messy quotes, project documents, supplier/client communication, repeat questions, SOPs, before/after proof or a clear need to turn scattered knowledge into a maintained operational context map.

## Best first five for a proof-led pilot

1. **Emerald Building Services** — strongest visible project/quote/trade-network pain and excellent local proof story.
2. **Emerald Buyers Agency** — directly overlaps BuyerProof AU and property decision support.
3. **CBW Accounting & Taxation** — document-heavy recurring client workflow, but privacy and professional-boundary review is essential.
4. **Emerald Detailing** — simple visual before/after proof and clear repeatable service workflow.
5. **OrgMent Business & Talent Solutions** — likely understands knowledge/process value and may be a referral or pilot partner.
