# Client Intake Checklist — Business Context Brain

## Fit questions

- What type of business/team is this for?
- What folder/process feels messiest?
- What do people ask repeatedly?
- What documents do you keep rewriting from scratch?
- What would be useful to find in 30 seconds?
- Who will use the finished vault?

## Source folder request

Ask for a bounded sample:

- 20–50 files for starter pilot, or
- 50–200 files for full sprint.

Good files:

- quotes/proposals,
- SOPs/checklists,
- meeting notes,
- PDFs,
- policy docs,
- project folders,
- templates,
- brainstorms,
- product/service docs.

Avoid initially:

- passwords/API keys,
- bank statements,
- medical/client-identifying records unless a privacy agreement/workflow exists,
- live credentials,
- highly sensitive HR/legal files.

## Permission and privacy

- [ ] Confirm who owns the documents.
- [ ] Confirm whether files can be copied into a working vault.
- [ ] Confirm whether personal/client info needs redaction.
- [ ] Confirm whether outputs may be used in anonymised case study.
- [ ] Confirm whether AI/cloud tools are allowed or whether local-only is required.

## Delivery preferences

- Obsidian vault?
- Plain Markdown folder?
- Google Drive/Notion export?
- AI-ready context pack only?
- Walkthrough video?
- Maintenance plan?
