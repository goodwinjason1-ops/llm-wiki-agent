# Business Context Brain

## Hero

### Your business has the knowledge. It is just trapped in messy files.

We turn your scattered documents, quotes, SOPs, project folders, brainstorms, PDFs, screenshots, and meeting notes into a clean AI-readable knowledge vault your team can search, browse, and reuse.

**A persistent business context system — not another AI chat that forgets everything tomorrow.**

## Problem

Most businesses already have the answers somewhere:

- old quotes,
- supplier docs,
- client briefs,
- project plans,
- SOPs,
- meeting notes,
- policy docs,
- brainstorms,
- spreadsheets,
- training material.

But the knowledge is scattered. Staff ask the same questions. Quotes get rebuilt from scratch. New team members need repeated explanations. AI tools underperform because the context is messy.

## Offer

### Context Map Sprint

In 7–10 days, we turn a messy folder of business knowledge into a structured Markdown/Obsidian-style vault with:

- source inventory,
- summaries of key documents,
- linked pages for people, projects, services, processes, clients, suppliers, and decisions,
- dashboards your team can start from,
- templates for future notes,
- an AI-ready context pack for Claude/Codex/Hermes or your preferred AI assistant.

## What you get

1. **Business Context Dashboard** — the starting page for your company knowledge.
2. **Document map** — what exists, what matters, what is missing.
3. **Linked knowledge pages** — reusable context instead of buried files.
4. **Templates** — quote summary, SOP, project note, meeting note, decision log.
5. **AI-ready context pack** — so future AI work starts with real company knowledge.
6. **Maintenance workflow** — how to keep the brain alive.
