# Demo Walkthrough Script — Business Context Brain

## 60-second version

"Most businesses already have the knowledge they need — it is just scattered across quotes, PDFs, SOPs, project folders, meeting notes, brainstorms, and staff memory.

This is a Business Context Brain. We take those messy files and turn them into a structured knowledge vault.

Here is the before: a laptop full of unrelated folders — basketball app code, trading bot docs, course material, business ideas, prototypes, YouTube research.

Here is the after: one dashboard that maps every project, classifies what is code versus knowledge, links related notes, creates safety rules, creates project instructions for AI tools, and gives us reusable workflows.

The key is persistence. Your AI assistant does not start from zero every time. Your team does not dig through folders. The business gets a memory."

## 5-minute walkthrough

### 1. Show the messy source world

Open/display:

- Desktop project folders,
- document/course folders,
- code repos,
- downloads/reference folders.

Say:

> "This is normal. Every business has this — old quotes, docs, screenshots, project folders, ideas, policies, spreadsheets. The problem is not lack of knowledge. The problem is lack of structure."

### 2. Show the project registry

Open:

`00_System/Dashboards/AI Second Brain Project Registry.md`

Say:

> "The first step is inventory. We classify what exists, where it lives, what type it is, and what safety rules apply."

### 3. Show per-project context notes

Open:

`05_Projects/Project Context Registry/`

Say:

> "Each folder gets a context note so future humans and AI tools can quickly understand what it is before touching anything."

### 4. Show Claude/project instructions

Open:

- basketball-pwa `CLAUDE.md`,
- Prediction Bot `CLAUDE.md`,
- Airdrop Agent `CLAUDE.md`.

Say:

> "For code or high-risk projects, we create operating rules. This prevents AI from breaking features, reading secrets, or doing unsafe trading/crypto actions."

### 5. Show dashboard/status

Open:

`00_System/Dashboards/Fable Extraction Master Status.md`

Say:

> "This is the operating dashboard. It shows what is extracted, what is blocked, and what to do next."

### 6. Product close

Say:

> "This is what we can do for a business. You give us the messy folder. We return a working context brain: dashboards, summaries, links, workflows, templates, and AI-ready instructions."
