# Video and Source Ingestion Module

## Why this matters

Many businesses have knowledge trapped in videos, webinars, Loom recordings, training calls, sales calls, podcasts, and YouTube playlists. A Business Context Brain should not only ingest static PDFs and docs; it should turn video/audio sources into reusable notes, SOPs, FAQs, training material, and workflows.

## Demo proof from Jayse's system

The YouTube transcript API initially failed on several AI-business videos. The fallback method worked:

1. use `yt-dlp --write-auto-subs`,
2. convert subtitles to transcript text,
3. save raw transcript under `02_Raw/youtube/transcripts/`,
4. create source summary under `03_Sources/youtube/`,
5. synthesize implications into the product/business project notes.

## Product promise

> Your team's videos should not disappear into a recordings folder. We turn calls, trainings, demos, and webinars into linked operational knowledge.

## Example outputs

- meeting/call summary,
- FAQ extraction,
- SOP draft,
- training module outline,
- decision log,
- client/project context note,
- reusable prompt/context pack for AI tools.

## Safety/privacy rules

- Get permission before ingesting meeting/client recordings.
- Redact private or sensitive details where needed.
- Do not treat transcripts as perfect; mark uncertain points as TODO verify.
- Regulated/clinical/legal/financial discussions remain reference context only, not professional advice.
