## Reusable scope snippets

### Bathroom demolition and removal

**Use when:** full or partial bathroom renovation.

```text
Allow to carefully isolate and remove nominated bathroom fixtures, fittings, vanity, shower screen, selected wall/floor linings, and associated debris from the work area. Works include reasonable protection of adjacent areas and disposal of standard renovation waste generated by the agreed scope.
```

**Clarifying question before using:**

- Are there asbestos-risk materials, unusually heavy fixtures, access limitations, or strata/body-corporate disposal requirements?

**Common exclusions:**

- asbestos testing/removal,
- structural rectification discovered after demolition,
- out-of-hours works,
- hidden water damage or termite damage,
- disposal of owner-supplied unrelated rubbish.

### Waterproofing inclusion

**Use when:** bathrooms, laundries, wet areas, balconies where relevant.

```text
Allow to prepare nominated wet-area surfaces and apply waterproofing system to relevant areas in accordance with product requirements and applicable Australian Standards for the agreed wet-area scope. Includes standard curing allowance before tiling or next trade stage.
```

**Risk note:** high. Client should understand curing time and that extra preparation may be required if substrate condition is poor.

**Variation trigger:** substrate not suitable, water damage, non-standard falls, previous waterproofing failure, incompatible product residue.

### Tile selection / tile variation clause

```text
Quote assumes standard-format tiles within the nominated allowance and normal laying pattern. Large-format, natural stone, mosaic, herringbone, complex patterns, rectification of uneven walls/floors, or tiles requiring specialist sealing/handling may alter labour, preparation, wastage, and material costs.
```

**Why this matters:** tile selection often changes after quoting and can create margin loss if the quote wording is too broad.

### Client supplied fixtures clause

```text
Where fixtures, fittings, tiles, appliances, or accessories are supplied by the client, the quote excludes delays, rework, warranty issues, missing parts, incorrect sizing, non-compliant items, or additional installation work caused by those supplied items unless specifically stated.
```

**Follow-up question:** who checks compatibility before order?

## Exclusions library

| Exclusion | Why it matters | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden structural damage | impossible to know pre-demo | "Rectification of hidden damage is excluded unless stated." |
| Asbestos/hazardous material | legal/safety risk | "Testing/removal by licensed provider excluded." |
| Owner-supplied item delays | timeline risk | "Delays caused by late/incompatible owner-supplied items may affect schedule/cost." |
| Permit/strata approvals | admin/time risk | "Approvals excluded unless specifically included." |
| Electrical/plumbing upgrades outside nominated scope | margin risk | "Code upgrades or additional works outside nominated fixtures excluded." |

## Variation/risk checklist

Before finalising a quote, check:

- [ ] Is the property occupied during works?
- [ ] Are access, parking, lift, or waste constraints known?
- [ ] Any strata/body-corporate rules?
- [ ] Any asbestos-age property risk?
- [ ] Are fixtures/tiles client supplied?
- [ ] Are tile size/pattern/sealing requirements confirmed?
- [ ] Are hidden-damage exclusions clear?
- [ ] Are waterproofing/curing timelines explained?
- [ ] Are allowances clearly separated from fixed inclusions?
- [ ] Has the follow-up email explained next steps and decision deadline?
