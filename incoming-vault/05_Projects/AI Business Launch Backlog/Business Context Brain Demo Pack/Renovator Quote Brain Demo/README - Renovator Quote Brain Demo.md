# Renovator Quote Brain Demo

## Purpose

This is the first vertical wedge for **Source-to-System Studio / Business Context Brain**.

Instead of selling a vague "AI second brain", this demo sells a concrete business outcome:

> We turn a renovator/builder's old quotes, scopes, supplier PDFs, project notes, photos, and follow-up messages into a reusable Quote Brain that helps them quote faster, reuse proven wording, spot variation risks, and onboard admin/estimators.

## Target buyer

Primary:

- bathroom renovators,
- kitchen renovators,
- small builders,
- landscapers,
- roofing contractors,
- pool builders,
- solar/battery installers,
- any high-ticket trade where quotes contain valuable repeated scope language.

Best-fit business signs:

- A$10k+ job values,
- many custom quotes,
- owner still involved in quoting,
- old quotes scattered across Drive/Dropbox/email/accounting software,
- admin/estimator onboarding pain,
- repeated customer objections,
- variation disputes or unclear exclusions.
