## Quote review workflow

### Step 1 — Scope clarity

- [ ] Is the work area clearly named?
- [ ] Is demolition/removal specific?
- [ ] Are supply vs install responsibilities clear?
- [ ] Are allowances separated from fixed inclusions?

### Step 2 — Risk control

- [ ] Hidden damage exclusion included?
- [ ] Hazardous material/asbestos note included where relevant?
- [ ] Access constraints documented?
- [ ] Client-supplied item clause included?
- [ ] Variation process explained?

### Step 3 — Customer understanding

- [ ] Is there a plain-English summary?
- [ ] Are exclusions visible, not buried?
- [ ] Are next steps clear?
- [ ] Has follow-up been scheduled?
