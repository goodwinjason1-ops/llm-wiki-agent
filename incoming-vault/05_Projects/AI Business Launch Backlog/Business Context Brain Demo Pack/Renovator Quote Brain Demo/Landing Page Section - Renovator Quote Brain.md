## The 7-day sprint

### Day 1 — Intake
You send 20–50 old/sample quotes, scope docs, supplier PDFs, project notes, and example follow-ups. Sensitive details can be redacted.

### Days 2–4 — Extraction
We extract reusable quote language, scope categories, inclusions/exclusions, risks, variations, supplier/material references, project examples, and follow-up patterns.

### Days 5–6 — Build
We organise everything into a Markdown/Obsidian-style vault and create your Quote Brain dashboard.

### Day 7 — Walkthrough
You get a walkthrough, example workflows, and an AI-ready context pack.
