# Outreach Messages — Renovator Quote Brain

## Positioning

Do not pitch "AI automation" first.

Pitch the pain:

- quotes take too long,
- old scope wording is hard to reuse,
- exclusions and variations are inconsistent,
- the owner is still the bottleneck,
- new admin/estimators need repeated explanations.

## Short email — owner/operator renovator

Subject: Could your old quotes become a quoting system?

Hi [Name],

Quick idea for [Business]: you probably have years of useful quoting knowledge buried in old quotes, scope docs, supplier PDFs, project notes, and follow-up emails.

I’m building a service called **Renovator Quote Brain**. It turns that messy archive into a reusable quoting knowledge base: scope wording, inclusions/exclusions, variation risks, follow-up templates, and an AI-ready estimator context pack.

It is not another generic chatbot. It is a way to reuse what your business has already learned.

If you send 3 anonymised quotes or scope docs, I can show you a small sample of what could be extracted before we talk about a paid sprint.

Worth a look?

— Jayse

## Phone opener

"I’m not calling about ads or a website. I’m testing a service that takes the quoting knowledge already inside a renovator’s old quotes and turns it into a reusable quote library and risk checklist. The first step is just 3 anonymised quote docs so I can show what can be extracted. Who normally handles quoting for you?"

## Objection handling

### "We already have templates."

That is a good sign. This is not replacing templates. It pulls the best wording and lessons from real quotes/projects into a cleaner library, especially the inclusions, exclusions, and variation risks that templates often miss.

### "We cannot share client information."

You can redact names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and prices. For the pilot sample I only need the structure and wording, not private details.

### "We use [ServiceM8/Buildertrend/Xero/Google Drive/etc.]."

Perfect. This does not need to replace those systems. It sits beside them as the reusable knowledge layer.

### "We are too busy."

That is exactly why the first step is tiny: send 3 anonymised documents. I’ll do the extraction and show whether it is worth a bigger sprint.

### "Is this AI?"

AI helps with extraction and organisation, but the deliverable is a human-readable quote knowledge base your team can use without being locked into a chatbot.

## Qualification questions

1. How many custom quotes do you send per month?
2. Who prepares or reviews quotes?
3. Where are old quotes and scopes stored?
4. Do you reuse scope wording from old jobs?
5. What quote mistakes or misunderstandings happen repeatedly?
6. Do clients often change fixtures/tiles/scope after quote?
7. Is onboarding a new admin/estimator painful?
8. Would a searchable quote language/risk library save time?
