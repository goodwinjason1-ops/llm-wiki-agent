## Three-minute walkthrough script

1. **Hook:** "Most small businesses don't need another chatbot; they need their context cleaned up so AI and staff can actually use it."
2. **Before:** show scattered folders/assets and explain the cost: repeated explanations, lost IP, slow onboarding, weak AI answers.
3. **Process:** inventory → raw preservation → source summaries → linked wiki/process notes → dashboard/action board.
4. **Proof:** show BuyerProof and Renovator Quote Brain assets mapped into a funnel rather than sitting as orphan docs.
5. **Offer:** "I can do a 7-10 day Context Map Sprint for your docs, quotes, SOPs, and project material."
6. **CTA:** "Send me 10-20 representative docs and I’ll map a small sample before you commit."
