# AI Business Video Opportunity Extraction - 2026-07-07

## Bottom line

The deeper video extraction **does not replace** BuyerProof AU or Business Context Brain. It changes the framing and adds several monetizable modules.

The strongest upgraded direction is:

# Source-to-System Studio

One-line offer:

> We turn your messy business sources — documents, quotes, SOPs, videos, meetings, brainstorms, project folders, and course material — into reusable systems: dashboards, SOP packs, quote libraries, training packs, templates, and AI-ready context.

This is broader and more commercial than saying only "second brain". A business buys the outcome: reusable systems, faster onboarding, better quotes, less repeated explanation, and AI that knows the company context.

## Extracted business models and workflows

### 1. Source-to-System Studio

**Description:** Turn scattered source material into usable business systems.

Input sources:

- documents,
- quote folders,
- project folders,
- SOPs,
- PDFs,
- videos,
- meeting recordings,
- course material,
- sales calls,
- brainstorms,
- old proposals.

Outputs:

- context vault,
- dashboards,
- SOPs,
- quote libraries,
- training packs,
- FAQs,
- onboarding packs,
- AI context files,
- reusable workflows.

Video support:

- NotebookLM video: build persistent systems that know niche, audience, and style permanently.
- Claude video skill: video is transcript + visual frames; source ingestion can include visual information, not just text.
- Digital product video: people pay for implementation, templates, scripts, systems, and support.

Best target customers:

- renovators/builders/trades,
- property businesses,
- small agencies,
- training providers,
- coaches/consultants,
- document-heavy small businesses.

First offer:

> Context Map Sprint / Source-to-System Sprint.

Price:

- pilot: A$750–A$1,500,
- standard: A$1,500–A$3,500,
- deeper buildout: A$4,000–A$10,000+.

### 2. Video Knowledge Pack

**Description:** Turn business videos/calls/trainings into operational knowledge.

Input:

- Looms,
- Zoom recordings,
- webinars,
- internal trainings,
- sales calls,
- YouTube videos,
- course libraries.

Output:

- transcript,
- source summary,
- SOP draft,
- FAQ,
- decision log,
- training module,
- action list,
- client/project context note.

Why it matters:

The Claude video skill transcript says normal transcript tools miss the visual side. The underlying product lesson is that **source ingestion quality matters**. Businesses do not only need text summarised; they need context extracted from real work artifacts.

First offer:

> Send 5–10 Looms/training videos. We turn them into SOPs, FAQs, and onboarding notes.

Price:

- A$500–A$2,000 depending on volume.

### 3. Reddit / Community Pain Mining Service

**Description:** Use Reddit/community scanning to find real customer pain before building an offer.

Video support:

The Reddit video says: do not brainstorm from a blank page; find real complaints people are already making in public. Look for:

- wish posts,
- complaint posts,
- tool-failure posts,
- repetitive-task complaints,
- expensive-service complaints,
- "does anyone know a better way" posts.

Output:

- pain-point map,
- exact customer language,
- existing solutions/gaps,
- offer ideas,
- pricing hypothesis,
- fast validation plan.

Use for us:

- validate Business Context Brain niches,
- validate BuyerProof product pain,
- identify best first customer segment,
- find language for landing pages/outreach.

First experiment:

Scan Reddit/communities for phrases like:

- "can't find SOPs",
- "quote template",
- "onboarding chaos",
- "lost client notes",
- "proposal template",
- "construction quote spreadsheet",
- "we have documents everywhere",
- "knowledge base is a mess",
- "Notion is chaos",
- "Google Drive mess".

**Description:** Turn the outputs of a Business Context Brain sprint into sellable or internal digital products.

Video support:

The digital product video strongly distinguishes between information and implementation:

- information is free,
- implementation is what people pay for,
- templates/scripts/step-by-step systems/support are the value.

Business Context Brain outputs can become:

- SOP pack,
- onboarding pack,
- quote library,
- training pack,

This is important because it gives the offer a concrete outcome beyond "a vault".

### 5. One-Person AI Service Business System

**Description:** Sell a narrow AI-enabled service before building software.

Video support:

The one-person business video emphasizes:

- get a high-paying client within 30 days,
- avoid technical rabbit holes,
- create an offer first,
- use the offer triangle:
  1. what are you selling?
  2. who are you selling to?
  3. what transformation/outcome do they get?
- sell before building.

Application:

Business Context Brain should not begin as SaaS. It should begin as:

> "I will map one messy business folder into a useful dashboard and context vault in 7–10 days."

## How this changes our plan

### Keep doing

1. **Business Context Brain** as the main commercial service experiment.
2. **BuyerProof AU** as a concrete consumer/productized asset line.
3. **Project registry / context mapping** as the internal proof demo.
4. **Video/source ingestion** as a visible feature.

### Add

1. Reposition Business Context Brain as **Source-to-System Studio** in sales language.
2. Add a **Video Knowledge Pack** offer.
3. Add **Reddit/community pain mining** before choosing first outreach niche.
4. Make the demo outcome more concrete:
   - not just "vault",
   - but "SOPs, quote libraries, onboarding packs, dashboards, and AI context".
5. Create a **renovator/builder quoting brain** as the first vertical demo.

### Defer

1. Building SaaS.
2. Generic AI receptionist/automation agency.
3. Faceless content as a primary business.
4. Trying to serve every type of business at once.
5. Clinical/regulated mental-health productization until privacy/ethics boundaries are clearer.

### Stop / avoid

1. Do not sell "AI second brain" as abstract tech.
2. Do not lead with Obsidian unless the buyer cares.
3. Do not overbuild internal systems before talking to prospects.
4. Do not let the product become a generic knowledge-base setup.
5. Do not offer regulated advice in AOD/mental health/property/trading contexts.

## Recommended first commercial experiment

# Renovator / Builder Quote Brain Sprint

This is the clearest niche application of Source-to-System Studio.

## Buyer

Small builders, bathroom/kitchen renovators, landscapers, solar/roofing/pool contractors, and high-ticket trades.

## Pain

- quotes rebuilt from scratch,
- scope wording inconsistent,
- supplier docs scattered,
- old projects hard to reference,
- variations not captured,
- photos and project notes not reused,
- onboarding admin/estimators is slow.

## Offer

> I turn your past quotes, scope docs, supplier PDFs, project notes, and photos into a reusable Quote Brain: a searchable library of scopes, inclusions/exclusions, common objections, project examples, and quote follow-up templates.

## Deliverables

- quote/source inventory,
- quote language library,
- inclusions/exclusions checklist,
- supplier/materials reference map,
- project case-study notes,
- variation/risk checklist,
- quote follow-up email/SMS templates,
- AI-ready estimator context pack,
- dashboard.

## Pilot price

A$750–A$1,500 for 20–50 sample docs.

## Standard price

A$2,000–A$5,000 depending on document volume and output depth.

## Validation plan

### Week 1

Build internal demo using Jayse folders + a fake/anonymized renovation quote-brain example.

### Week 2

Mine Reddit/Facebook/forums for renovator/builder quoting/document chaos language.

### Week 3

Outreach to 20 local renovators/builders/trades with a small pilot offer.

### Week 4

Deliver one pilot or get 5 discovery calls. Continue if:

- at least 2 serious conversations, or
- 1 paid pilot, or
- repeated pain language appears in replies.

Good for:

Risks:

Recommendation: promising, but second wave after Context Brain proof.
