# Tradie AI Receptionist Launch Plan

## Executive summary

The strongest first opportunity is a **local AI receptionist + simple website/conversion package for trade-based businesses**: plumbers, electricians, carpenters, builders, roofers, HVAC, landscapers, and similar one-man or small-team shops.

This is a better first target than dentists/clinics for Jayse because:

- Local tradies often miss calls while on tools, driving, quoting, or on job sites.
- A missed emergency plumbing/electrical call can be worth hundreds or thousands of dollars.
- Many have weak websites, slow follow-up, no proper intake, and inconsistent admin.
- They usually do not need a complex enterprise solution; they need calls answered, jobs qualified, and messages organized.
- Jayse can sell locally with trust, demos, and direct conversation rather than competing in broad AI agency markets.

## Positioning

### Working offer name

**Tradie CallMate**

Alternative names:

- Tradie Reception AI
- On-Tools Receptionist
- Local Job Booker AI
- Missed Call Mate
- After-Hours Tradie Desk

### One-line pitch

> “An AI receptionist for local tradies that answers missed calls, qualifies jobs, captures photos/details, and sends you clean job leads while you’re on the tools.”

### Core pain

Small tradies lose work because they cannot answer every call, especially during:

- active jobs,
- driving,
- after-hours emergencies,
- quoting days,
- family time,
- poor reception/job sites.

### Core promise

Not “replace your whole business admin.” Start narrower:

> “Never lose a good job lead just because you missed the call.”

## Target niches ranked

| Rank | Niche | Why | First angle |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plumbers | Urgent jobs, high call value, after-hours demand, photos/details useful | Emergency and quote intake |
| 2 | Electricians | Safety/urgency, good ticket sizes, clear qualification questions | Job qualification + callback priority |
| 3 | HVAC / aircon | Seasonal spikes, quote requests, maintenance plans | Booking and seasonal overflow |
| 4 | Roofers / guttering | Photo-based qualification, high-value jobs | Capture photos + job location |
| 5 | Builders / carpenters | Larger projects, more qualification needed | Filter serious enquiries from tyre-kickers |
| 6 | Landscapers | Many quote requests, visual/photo intake | Quote request capture and follow-up |

Recommended starting niche: **plumbers or electricians**.

## MVP package

### Version 1: AI missed-call and job-intake assistant

Must-have features:

1. **Phone call answering or missed-call text-back**
   - If full voice is too much initially, start with missed-call SMS automation.
   - Message: “Hey, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed you — can you tell us what job you need help with?”

2. **Job qualification flow**
   - Name
   - Phone/email
   - Suburb/postcode
   - Trade type/problem category
   - Emergency vs non-urgent
   - Photos/videos if possible
   - Preferred time
   - Budget/ownership/rental status if relevant

3. **Tradie summary notification**
   - Send clean summary by SMS/email/WhatsApp/Google Sheet.
   - Include urgency score and recommended callback priority.

4. **Simple admin dashboard**
   - Google Sheet or Airtable first; no need for full SaaS at MVP.
   - Columns: date, name, suburb, job type, urgency, value estimate, status, notes.

5. **Website widget or mini landing page**
   - “Request a quote” / “Emergency job?” button.
   - Works for tradies with no site or weak existing site.

### Optional V1.5 features

- FAQ answering: service area, hours, emergency fees, licence/insurance, typical call-out info.
- Booking calendar link.
- Photo upload form.
- Follow-up if customer does not reply.
- Review request automation after job completion.

### Do not include at first

- Full CRM rebuild.
- Complex calendar dispatch.
- Taking payments.
- Giving technical/safety advice.
- Promising exact quote prices.
- Fully autonomous scheduling without tradie approval.

## Packages and pricing

### Pilot offer

**Founding Tradie Pilot**

- Setup: **A$300–A$750**
- Monthly: **A$149–A$299/mo** for first 1–3 pilot clients
- In exchange: testimonial, feedback, permission to use anonymized case study.

### Standard packages after proof

| Package | Price | Includes | Best for |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Missed Call Saver | A$299 setup + A$199/mo | Missed-call SMS, intake form, lead sheet, notifications | One-man shops |
| AI Reception Starter | A$750 setup + A$399/mo | Chat/voice intake, website widget, lead dashboard, weekly report | Busy small tradies |
| Website + AI Reception | A$1,500–A$2,500 setup + A$499–A$799/mo | One-page website, AI intake, missed-call workflow, basic SEO/local landing page | Weak/no website tradies |

Pricing logic:

- If one extra job per month covers the fee, the pitch is realistic.
- For emergency plumbers/electricians, one saved job could cover several months.
- Keep the first sale simple: “Let’s catch the jobs you’re currently missing.”

## 8-week milestone plan

### Phase 0 — Setup and offer definition

**Timeline:** 2–3 days

Deliverables:

- Choose first niche: plumbers or electricians.
- Choose offer name and one-liner.
- Define intake questions for the niche.
- Create a simple demo script.
- Decide MVP stack.

Suggested MVP stack:

- Landing page: simple static site or Carrd/Webflow/Framer/Next.js.
- Intake form: Tally/Typeform/custom form.
- Automation: n8n/Zapier/Make or a small custom script.
- Lead database: Google Sheets or Airtable.
- Notifications: email + SMS/WhatsApp where practical.
- AI layer: controlled FAQ/intake summarizer, not unrestricted advice bot.

Exit criteria:

- One clear niche.
- One-page offer.
- Demo flow mapped from call/text → intake → tradie summary.

### Phase 1 — Build demo MVP

**Timeline:** Week 1

Deliverables:

1. Demo website page for a fictional local plumber/electrician.
2. Intake assistant flow.
3. Lead summary output.
4. Google Sheet/Airtable lead tracker.
5. 60–90 second Loom demo.
6. Short demo script for outreach.

Demo scenario examples:

- “Burst pipe after hours.”
- “Need a quote for switchboard upgrade.”
- “Blocked drain, customer has photos.”
- “Leaking roof after rain.”

Exit criteria:

- Jayse can show a prospect the system in under 2 minutes.
- Test lead appears in dashboard and sends a clean notification.

### Phase 2 — Local lead list and validation

**Timeline:** Week 2

Deliverables:

- 100 local tradie leads.
- Score each lead by:
  - bad/no website,
  - no online booking,
  - mobile number only,
  - emergency/high-value service,
  - poor response/review complaints about communication,
  - local proximity/trust.

Validation actions:

- Contact 30–50 leads.
- Call or visit 10 highest-fit businesses.
- Ask simple discovery questions:
  - “Do you miss many calls while on jobs?”
  - “Do customers text photos/details?”
  - “How do you keep track of quote requests?”
  - “What happens after hours?”
  - “What would one extra booked job per month be worth?”

Exit criteria:

- 5+ conversations with target tradies.
- 2+ say the pain is real and agree to see a demo.
- At least 1 pilot candidate.

### Phase 3 — First pilot

**Timeline:** Weeks 3–4

Deliverables:

- Configure the system for one real tradie.
- Use their business name, service area, services, hours, emergency policy, and preferred contact method.
- Route leads to their phone/email/sheet.
- Add simple weekly report.

Pilot scope:

- Run for 14 days.
- Focus on capturing missed/new enquiries, not fully replacing admin.
- Manually review every AI summary at first if needed.

Success metrics:

- Number of leads captured.
- Number of missed calls recovered.
- Response time improvement.
- Number of qualified quote/job opportunities.
- Tradie satisfaction: “Would you keep paying for this?”

Exit criteria:

- One working pilot.
- One testimonial or at least permission to create anonymized case study.
- Clear notes on what broke/confused users.

### Phase 4 — Case study and repeatable package

**Timeline:** Weeks 5–6

Deliverables:

- Case study page.
- Before/after video.
- Refined pricing.
- Repeatable onboarding checklist.
- Reusable niche FAQ/intake template.
- Outreach script v2.

Content assets from pilot:

- “How a local plumber can stop losing jobs to missed calls.”
- “I built an AI receptionist for a tradie in 48 hours.”
- “What happens when a customer calls after hours?”
- “Before/after: messy enquiry vs clean job summary.”

Exit criteria:

- Offer can be explained, demoed, sold, and installed repeatedly.
- Start selling standard package rather than custom one-off builds.

### Phase 5 — Second and third clients

**Timeline:** Weeks 7–8

Deliverables:

- 2–3 paying clients or a clear pivot signal.
- Service delivery checklist.
- Support process.
- Monthly report template.
- Basic website/landing page for Jayse’s service.

Exit criteria:

- A$500–A$1,500 MRR target from early clients.
- Proof that the offer can be sold outside friends/family.
- Decide whether to specialize by niche or expand across local trades.

## Sales approach

### Best first channel

Local direct outreach beats ads/content initially.

Priority order:

1. Existing local network/referrals.
2. Phone calls to businesses with weak websites.
3. In-person visits for nearby tradies/suppliers.
4. Local Facebook/community groups.
5. YouTube/Instagram content as credibility support.

### Outreach opener

> “Hey, I’m local and I’m testing a simple AI receptionist for tradies. It catches missed calls and turns them into clean job summaries while you’re on the tools. I’m looking for 2–3 local plumbers/electricians to trial it at a founding rate. Want me to show you a 60-second demo?”

### Discovery questions

- How many calls do you miss in a normal week?
- Do you lose jobs when you cannot answer straight away?
- Do customers send photos or messy text messages?
- What details do you always need before deciding if a job is worth quoting?
- Do you already have a website/contact form?
- Would a clean job summary by SMS/email be useful?
- What would one extra booked job per month be worth?

## YouTube/digital-product angle

The YouTube/digital-product videos are viable, but as a **second layer**, not the first revenue engine.

### Viable digital product ideas from the folders

| Rank | Digital product | Why viable | How it connects to tradie offer |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tradie Missed-Call Script Pack** | Simple, cheap, fast to make | Entry-level product for DIY tradies |
| 2 | **AI Receptionist Setup Template** | Good for small business owners/AI agency beginners | Productized version of our internal process |
| 3 | **Local Business AI Intake Form Templates** | Useful across plumbers/electricians/builders | Lead magnet or low-ticket product |
| 4 | **Tradie Website Copy + FAQ Pack** | Helps weak/no-website tradies | Upsell into done-for-you website |
| 5 | **YouTube Shorts/Reels Content Calendar for Tradies** | Helps clients market themselves | Add-on service or digital product |
| 6 | **AI Automation Starter Kit for Local Trades** | Broader but still niche | Could become $29–$99 product after proof |

### Recommended digital-product ladder

Do not start with a big course. Start small:

1. **Free lead magnet** — “10 missed-call text templates for tradies.”
2. **Low-ticket product** — A$19–A$49 “Tradie AI Receptionist Starter Kit.”
3. **Mid-ticket template pack** — A$99–A$199 “Local Business AI Intake + Follow-Up System.”
4. **Done-for-you service** — A$750+ setup and A$299+/mo.

### YouTube content strategy

Use YouTube to sell trust and templates, not rely on AdSense.

Initial content pillars:

1. **Build-in-public demos**
   - “I built an AI receptionist for a local plumber.”
   - “Here’s what happens when a customer calls after hours.”

2. **Tradie growth/ops tips**
   - “The simple missed-call text that saves jobs.”
   - “Why your contact form is losing you work.”

3. **AI templates/tutorials**
   - “How to create a quote intake form with AI.”
   - “How to turn messy customer messages into clean job summaries.”

4. **Case studies**
   - “Before/after: from missed call to booked quote.”

Posting plan:

- 2 Shorts/Reels per week from demos and tips.
- 1 longer YouTube video every 2 weeks once there is a pilot/story.
- Every video links to the free lead magnet and service enquiry.

## Key risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Tradies do not trust AI answering calls | Start with missed-call text-back and lead summaries, then upgrade to voice |
| AI gives bad technical/safety advice | Constrain it to intake, FAQs, and routing; no diagnosis or pricing promises |
| Too much custom work | Use niche-specific templates and onboarding checklist |
| Low willingness to pay | Anchor against one saved job per month; start with pilots |
| Platform/content takes too long | Use direct outreach first; content supports trust |
| Voice/SMS costs eat margin | Price monthly plans with usage tiers |
| Existing websites are poor | Offer website + AI package as higher setup tier |

## Competition-aware adjustment

Follow-up market research found that the Australian AI receptionist for tradies niche is already active. Direct competitors include tradie-specific services such as Johnni.ai, AppyTradies, Merit, Square AI, Chime Labs, Skedy, Deskie, TransferToAI, TradeMate AI, TradieDee, ADA Assist, plus broader AI receptionist tools and human virtual receptionist services.

Strategic implication:

- Do **not** position as a generic “AI receptionist for tradies.”
- Position as a **local missed-call recovery + website/contact-flow cleanup service** for nearby sole-trader plumbers/electricians.
- Start with a low-risk 14-day pilot that catches missed calls and sends clean job summaries.
- Compete on local trust, hands-on setup, first-month human review/tuning, website fixes, and local case studies — not on cheapest software price.

Recommended revised wedge:

> “I’ll set up missed-call recovery on your current number, ask the right job questions, text you clean summaries, and fix the enquiry path on your website. We test it for 14 days and see if it catches jobs you would have missed.”
