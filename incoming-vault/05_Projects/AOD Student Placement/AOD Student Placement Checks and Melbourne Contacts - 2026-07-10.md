# AOD Student Placement Checks and Melbourne Contacts - 2026-07-10

## Bottom line

For AOD student placement in Melbourne/Victoria, the most commonly requested checks found in placement/provider sources are:

1. **National Police Check / National Police Certificate**
2. **Working with Children Check** where the placement may involve people under 18
3. **NDIS Worker Screening Check** only where the placement is in an NDIS risk-assessed role or the host requires it

For Jayse, the practical move is to ask the course placement coordinator and/or host agency for:

- exact wording/purpose required on the police check,
- whether the host has a Victoria Police **Community Volunteer Fee (CVF) number** for the student-placement police-check discount,
- whether a **Working with Children Check** is required,
- whether an **NDIS Worker Screening Check** is required and, if yes, the host organisation's **Employer ID / Provider ID** needed to apply.

## Check 1 — National Police Check / National Police Certificate

Official sources:

- Victoria Police — Apply for a National Police Check: https://www.police.vic.gov.au/apply-national-police-check
- Victoria Police — Fees and charges: https://www.police.vic.gov.au/national-police-check-fees-charges
- Victoria Police — Police checks/fingerprinting enquiries: https://www.police.vic.gov.au/national-police-record-checks-fingerprinting-contact
- Service Victoria — National Police Check: https://service.vic.gov.au/services/national-police-check

Key details found:

- Victoria Police issues National Police Certificates for employment, volunteering and licensing.
- A National Police Check can be used for employment, volunteer work and **student placement**.
- Applications require at least **three identity documents totalling 100 points**, including one photo ID.
- Standard processing: allow at least **10 business days** for a National Police Check.
- Online applications through Service Victoria receive a PDF certificate via email; postal applications receive a physical certificate by post.
- If unsure which police check type is needed, ask the employer/placement organisation requesting the certificate.

Fees located on Victoria Police fee page:

- Standard fee: **$91.50**
- Reduced volunteer/student-placement fee: **$31.10**
- The reduced fee requires the organisation/education provider to supply/use a **Community Volunteer Fee (CVF) Number**.
- CVF number enquiries for organisations: `PES-CVF-MGR@police.vic.gov.au`
- Victoria Police postal address for applications/payments: Public Enquiry Service, Victoria Police, GPO Box 919, Melbourne VIC 3001.

Contact:

- Victoria Police police-check/fingerprinting phone: **1300 881 596**, Monday–Friday 8:30am–3:30pm excluding public holidays.
- Enquiry page: https://www.police.vic.gov.au/national-police-record-checks-fingerprinting-contact

Placement caution:

- Some universities/health placements require the police check purpose to cover supervised/unsupervised access to vulnerable people. Confirm wording with the course placement coordinator before applying.

## Check 2 — NDIS Worker Screening Check Victoria

Official sources:

- Victorian Government — NDIS Worker Screening Check: https://www.vic.gov.au/ndis-worker-screening-check
- Service Victoria — NDIS Worker Screening Check: https://service.vic.gov.au/find-services/work-and-volunteering/ndis-worker-screening-check
- NDIS Commission — Worker screening: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/workers/worker-screening

Key details found:

- The NDIS Worker Screening Check is for people working in **risk-assessed roles** with NDIS participants.
- Registered NDIS providers must identify their risk-assessed roles.
- "No clearance, no start" applies where an NDIS clearance is required for the risk-assessed role.
- Apply online through Service Victoria.
- The application requires:
  - Service Victoria account/login,
  - identity verification,
  - identity documents for the police/background checks,
  - work details,
  - the **Employer ID** of the individual or organisation requiring the NDIS Check,
  - consent and payment.
- The application must then be verified by the employer/organisation named in the application.
- After verification, assessment can take up to **3 weeks**, but may be shorter where there is no criminal history to assess.
- Clearance is valid for **5 years** across Australia unless surrendered or revoked.
- Current Victorian Government page listed fee: **$139.20**.
- Volunteer fee waiver exists for eligible volunteer risk-assessed roles, but the fee-waiver PDF states tertiary students on work experience/work placement are **not eligible for a volunteer fee waiver**.

Contact details found:

- Service Victoria customer support: **1300 652 879** or **132 VIC (132 842)**.
- NDIS Worker Screening email found in Service Victoria/Victorian material: `NDISworkerscreening@justice.vic.gov.au` and `ndisworkerscreening@dgs.vic.gov.au` appears on refund material; use the current Vic page/contact channel first.
- NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission general line found in volunteer waiver material: **1800 035 544**.

Practical warning:

- Do not apply for the NDIS check blindly. You generally need the placement host's Employer ID / Provider ID and their verification. Ask the placement provider first.

## Related check — Working with Children Check

Source:

- Victorian Government — Working with Children Check: https://www.vic.gov.au/working-with-children-check

Why it matters:

- A Police Check and WWCC are different.
- A Police Check does not allow you to do child-related work in Victoria.
- A WWCC may be required if placement involves children/young people. Odyssey and Turning Point placement pages specifically mention Police Check + WWCC for student placements.

## Recommended next actions for Jayse

1. Ask your course placement coordinator for a placement-prep checklist and whether they partner with Odyssey, Turning Point, cohealth, EACH, SHARC, Uniting/ReGen, Windana, YSAS, or health-service placements.
2. Ask whether your police check must state a particular purpose, e.g. student placement / vulnerable people / health/community setting.
3. Ask the university/host for a **CVF number** before paying for the National Police Check so you can use the student-placement reduced fee if eligible.
4. Apply for the National Police Check through Service Victoria/Victoria Police once the required wording/CVF is confirmed.
5. Apply for a WWCC if your course/placement may involve under-18s or if the host asks for it.
6. Only apply for the NDIS Worker Screening Check if the placement host confirms it is needed and gives you the Employer ID / Provider ID for verification.
7. Start with organisations that explicitly mention students: Odyssey, Turning Point, SHARC, cohealth, EACH; then broaden to Windana, Uniting/ReGen, YSAS, First Step, BHN/Star Health, Monash/SECADA, Eastern Health, St Vincent’s/Nexus.
