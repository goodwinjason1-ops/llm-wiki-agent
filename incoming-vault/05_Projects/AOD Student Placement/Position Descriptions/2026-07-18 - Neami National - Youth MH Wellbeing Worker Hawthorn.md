## Position overview

Tailored support to vulnerable young people living with multiple complex needs including mental health challenges, complex trauma, dual diagnosis, history of family violence, dual disability, gender dysphoria and eating disorders. Uses a developmentally appropriate informed framework with goal-focused coaching, connection to services, psychosocial group programs, and shared/individual support during residential stays.

Neami National supports 30,000+ people living with mental health challenges across Australia over 40 different Indigenous lands, offering services in mental health and wellbeing, housing and homelessness, and suicide prevention.

## Key responsibilities

- Support young people with multiple complex needs using a developmentally appropriate informed framework
- Goal-focused coaching
- Connection to other relevant services and supports
- Facilitation of psychosocial group programs
- Shared and individual support of young people during residential stay
