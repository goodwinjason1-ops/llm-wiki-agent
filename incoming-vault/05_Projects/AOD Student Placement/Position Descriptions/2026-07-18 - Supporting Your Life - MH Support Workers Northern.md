## Position overview

People-centred mental health service provider working within a person-centred, recovery-oriented framework. Providing mental health support work and psychosocial recovery coaching. Services delivered in-home, in community, and one-on-one. Located in Melbourne, seeking support workers for Northern Suburbs area.

## Key responsibilities

- Provide one-on-one support to people, increasing their capacity to thrive in the community
- Deliver mental health support work and psychosocial recovery coaching
- Work within a person-centred, recovery-oriented framework
- Support people with psychosocial disabilities in-home and in community settings

## Qualifications and certification required

- Certificate IV in Mental Health minimum
- Experienced in delivering mental health support in a recovery-oriented environment
- NDIS Workers Screening Check (or willing to obtain)
- Working with Children's Check (or willing to obtain)
- Driver's license, clean and reliable vehicle, and comprehensive insurance
- First Aid and CPR certification (or willing to obtain)
