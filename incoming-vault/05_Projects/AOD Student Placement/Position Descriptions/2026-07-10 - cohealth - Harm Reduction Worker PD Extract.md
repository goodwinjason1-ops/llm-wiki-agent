## Position overview

The role sits in cohealth's **AOD CBD Response cluster**, aiming to improve the health, wellbeing, housing and social outcomes of people who use alcohol and other drugs in the Melbourne CBD.

The City Street Health Team provides assertive street outreach and case management in the CBD, particularly with people who use substances and are experiencing homelessness.

The role is described as **hybrid** and provides harm reduction support across:

- outreach;
- drop-in;
- concierge;
- Needle & Syringe Program functions.

The PD states that the time dedicated to each function will vary depending on service need and Program Facilitator direction.

## Key duties

- Support 244 Flinders St drop-in space, including meal preparation, community development, Harm Reduction programs, group activities and health promotion.
- Support 244 Flinders St concierge area to create a safe and welcoming environment.
- Undertake assertive street outreach within a multidisciplinary team.
- Provide harm reduction education, advocacy, referral, needles/syringes and injecting/disposal equipment.
- Support safer using and disposal across onsite, outreach and dispensing-machine modalities.
- Undertake community engagement/development to reduce stigma and promote community safety/wellbeing.
- Help clients navigate cohealth and external services, including housing, social inclusion, recreation, recovery, treatment and pharmacotherapy.
- Provide safer drug use, BBV/STI, overdose prevention and response education.
- Work safely with intoxicated consumers and assess/respond within scope to overdose risk.
- Respond calmly/assertively to challenging behaviour using a trauma-informed framework.

## Key selection criteria

- Minimum Certificate IV in a relevant discipline, e.g. AOD, Community Development, Social Work.
- Lived experience highly regarded.
- Sound knowledge of health/social issues and the service sector for people who use substances and sleep rough.
- Ability to assess client need and develop a care plan to support navigation of cohealth and external services.
- Strong belief and personal alignment with cohealth values and mission.

## Checks / requirements

- WWCC.
- Nationally Coordinated Criminal History Check.
- Immunisation Category A / vaccination evidence.
