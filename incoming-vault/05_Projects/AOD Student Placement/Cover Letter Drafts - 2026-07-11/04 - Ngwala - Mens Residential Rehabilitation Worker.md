## Re: Men’s Residential Rehabilitation Worker — Alcohol and Other Drugs

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Men’s Residential Rehabilitation Worker position in St Kilda. I am attracted to the opportunity to provide reliable day-to-day support in a residential AOD environment while contributing to a safe, respectful and culturally responsive recovery setting.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. My training has developed my understanding of harm minimisation, trauma-informed and person-centred practice, recovery planning, co-occurring mental-health needs, risk awareness, professional boundaries and service navigation. I am keen to apply this foundation while learning from Ngwala’s Aboriginal community-controlled model of care.

I recognise that culturally safe practice requires humility, active listening, accountability and respect for Aboriginal self-determination. I would not assume cultural expertise that I do not hold. Instead, I would follow the guidance of Aboriginal colleagues and community, reflect on my practice, challenge stigma and discrimination, and work consistently within Ngwala’s values, policies and cultural-safety expectations.

My experience as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has built practical skills relevant to residential work: establishing rapport, supporting routines and participation, facilitating groups, managing changing group dynamics, maintaining safeguarding and behavioural boundaries, de-escalating conflict and communicating clearly with participants, families and staff. I understand the importance of dependable shift work, accurate documentation, effective handovers and calm responses when people are distressed or behaviour becomes challenging.

I hold a current Working with Children Check and Victorian driver’s licence and have access to a roadworthy vehicle. I completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025 and am willing to obtain a National Police Certificate meeting Ngwala’s recency requirements and provide any other required checks.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring my AOD qualification, developing mental-health knowledge and community-based support skills to Ngwala’s residential team. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,

**Jason Goodwin**
