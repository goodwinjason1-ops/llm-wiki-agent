## Re: Public Intoxication Response Assertive Outreach Worker

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the casual Public Intoxication Response Assertive Outreach Worker position in St Kilda. The opportunity to provide practical, non-judgmental and culturally safe support through a harm-minimisation approach strongly aligns with my AOD qualification and the direction in which I want to develop my practice.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. This training has developed my understanding of harm minimisation, intoxication and overdose risk, trauma-informed engagement, co-occurring mental-health needs, referral pathways, professional boundaries and working within scope. I understand that assertive outreach requires calm situational assessment, reliable teamwork, clear communication and respect for a person’s immediate safety, dignity and choices.

I recognise that culturally safe support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people must be led by respect for self-determination and Aboriginal community control. I would approach the role with humility, listen to community and Aboriginal colleagues, follow Ngwala’s cultural protocols, reflect on my own assumptions and practise in ways that reduce rather than reproduce stigma and discrimination.

My work as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has developed transferable skills in rapidly building rapport, reading group dynamics, supporting people experiencing distress, de-escalating conflict, maintaining safeguarding and behavioural boundaries, and coordinating effectively with other adults and services. I remain calm under pressure and understand the importance of dependable handovers and accurate incident documentation.

I hold a current Working with Children Check and Victorian driver’s licence, have access to a roadworthy vehicle, and completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025. I am willing to obtain a National Police Certificate issued within the required three-month period and complete any further checks or training.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Ngwala’s public-intoxication response while learning from an experienced culturally grounded outreach team. Thank you for considering my application.
