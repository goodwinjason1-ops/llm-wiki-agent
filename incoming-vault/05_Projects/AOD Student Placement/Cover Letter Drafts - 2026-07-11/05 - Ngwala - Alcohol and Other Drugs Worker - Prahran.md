## Re: Alcohol and Other Drugs Worker — Prahran

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Alcohol and Other Drugs Worker position in Prahran. I am strongly interested in Ngwala’s culturally safe outreach model and the opportunity to support Aboriginal people, families and communities affected by alcohol and other drug use.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. My formal training has given me a sound foundation in harm minimisation, trauma-informed and person-centred support, AOD and mental-health complexity, referral pathways, risk awareness, care-planning principles and professional boundaries. I am seeking an opportunity to develop this foundation through accountable frontline practice and experienced supervision.

I recognise that culturally safe AOD work within an Aboriginal community-controlled organisation requires more than goodwill. It requires respect for self-determination, listening to community, awareness of the continuing effects of colonisation and racism, willingness to reflect on power and bias, and accountability to Aboriginal leadership. I would approach the role with humility, follow cultural guidance and organisational protocols, and avoid claiming expertise or community authority I do not hold.

My experience as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has developed transferable strengths in building trust, mentoring, facilitating groups, supporting people through challenging situations, maintaining clear boundaries, safeguarding, de-escalating conflict and coordinating communication with families and colleagues. These experiences have also taught me to be consistent, practical and calm—qualities that are important in outreach and psychosocial support.

I hold a current Working with Children Check and Victorian driver’s licence, have access to a roadworthy vehicle, and completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025. I am willing to obtain a National Police Certificate issued within the required timeframe and meet all other employment checks.

I understand this role may require established outreach case-management experience beyond my current direct paid-sector experience. I would welcome an honest discussion about fit and am committed to working within scope, seeking supervision and continuing to build my capability.

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,
