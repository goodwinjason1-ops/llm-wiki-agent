## Re: Harm Reduction Peer Outreach Worker

Dear Ms McKinnon,

I am writing to apply for the part-time ongoing Harm Reduction Peer Outreach Worker position within North Richmond Community Health’s Outreach and Community Engagement team. The role’s commitment to meeting people where they are, reducing stigma and addressing health inequity strongly aligns with my values, AOD training and interest in practical harm reduction.

I bring relevant lived experience of alcohol and other drug use and recovery. I understand that peer practice is not simply sharing a personal story: it requires purposeful and boundaried use of lived experience, reflective practice, awareness of power and stigma, and a consistent focus on the other person’s goals. I would approach this responsibility with care, supervision and accountability.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. My training has strengthened my understanding of harm minimisation, trauma-informed and person-centred support, overdose risk, referral pathways, co-occurring mental-health needs and the structural barriers experienced by people who use drugs. I am particularly motivated by the opportunity to contribute to assertive outreach, mobile NSP and naloxone education, practical support and shared care coordination.

My experience as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has developed highly transferable skills in rapport-building, group facilitation, safeguarding, de-escalation, maintaining boundaries and working collaboratively with participants, families and staff. I have learned to remain calm, communicate without judgement and support engagement even when trust takes time.

NRCH’s values of Working Together, Being Courageous, Embracing Diversity and Making a Difference resonate strongly with me. I would value the opportunity to contribute to a multidisciplinary team that recognises lived experience as professional expertise while providing external supervision, reflective practice and continuing development.

I hold a current Working with Children Check, Victorian driver’s licence and First Aid/CPR completed through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025. I am willing to complete the required criminal-history and immunisation checks.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my lived experience, qualifications and community-based skills could contribute to NRCH’s harm-reduction outreach work.

Yours sincerely,

**Jason Goodwin**
