## Re: AOD Support Worker — Eastern Bridge Residential Rehabilitation Program

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the casual AOD Support Worker position at the Eastern Bridge Residential Rehabilitation Program in The Basin. I am strongly drawn to the opportunity to provide practical and emotional support within a residential recovery environment grounded in hope, compassion and dignity.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. This training has developed my understanding of harm minimisation, trauma-informed and person-centred practice, recovery-oriented support, risk awareness, professional boundaries and the interaction between substance use, mental health and social disadvantage. I am keen to apply that foundation while learning from experienced residential AOD practitioners.

The role’s emphasis on program engagement, daily routines, group participation and safe community interaction strongly matches my transferable experience as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator. In those positions I have built rapport, facilitated groups, supported participants through challenging moments, maintained safeguarding and behavioural boundaries, de-escalated conflict, communicated with families and staff, and helped create consistent and inclusive environments. I understand that effective residential support also depends on reliability, clear handovers, accurate records and calm responses to incidents.

I hold a current Employee Working with Children Check, Victorian driver’s licence and access to a roadworthy vehicle. I completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025 and am willing to obtain a current Nationally Coordinated Criminal History Check and provide any required evidence before commencement.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my AOD qualification, developing mental-health capability and strengths in rapport, group support and calm communication to the Eastern Bridge team. Thank you for considering my application.
