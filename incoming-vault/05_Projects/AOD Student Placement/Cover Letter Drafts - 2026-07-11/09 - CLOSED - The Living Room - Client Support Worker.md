## Re: Client Support Worker — The Living Room

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in future Client Support Worker opportunities with The Living Room. I am strongly drawn to low-barrier services that provide respectful, trauma-informed assistance to people experiencing homelessness alongside alcohol and other drug or mental-health challenges.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. My training has developed my understanding of harm minimisation, trauma-informed and person-centred engagement, brief intervention, service navigation, co-occurring needs, risk awareness and professional boundaries. I value practical support that starts with a person’s immediate priorities and preserves dignity and choice.

My experience as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has strengthened my ability to build rapport quickly, listen without judgement, facilitate groups, respond calmly to distress, de-escalate conflict, maintain safeguarding and behavioural boundaries, and coordinate communication with participants, families and colleagues. I understand that a busy drop-in environment requires warmth and flexibility alongside consistent limits, teamwork, accurate records and attention to safety.

I would be motivated to support clients with brief interventions, referrals, practical assistance and connection to health, housing and community services. I am committed to avoiding assumptions, working within scope and engaging respectfully with people whose experiences of services may include exclusion, stigma or mistrust.

I hold a current Working with Children Check and Victorian driver’s licence, have access to a roadworthy vehicle, and completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025. I am willing to complete all required police and employment checks.

Thank you for considering my interest in future suitable Client Support Worker opportunities.
