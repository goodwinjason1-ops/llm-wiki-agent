## Re: AOD Peer Support Worker — Coburg

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the permanent part-time AOD Peer Support Worker position based in Coburg. I am motivated by the opportunity to support and empower people using AOD services across Inner North and Northwest Victoria while contributing a lived-experience perspective alongside the STAR Intake team and clinical staff.

I bring relevant lived experience of alcohol and other drug use, treatment and recovery. I understand the importance of using lived experience deliberately rather than making it the centre of another person’s support. My approach would be strengths-based, recovery-oriented and respectful of each person’s right to define their own goals, with clear professional boundaries and regular reflection and supervision.

I have completed a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs and am currently studying the Diploma of Mental Health. This has developed my knowledge of harm minimisation, trauma-informed practice, co-occurring AOD and mental-health needs, referral pathways, risk awareness and person-centred engagement. I would value the opportunity to complement clinical care through peer connection, practical encouragement and advocacy while contributing lived-experience insights to service improvement.

My work as a basketball coach and Coach Coordinator has strengthened my ability to develop trust, facilitate groups, mentor others, manage group dynamics, maintain safeguarding and behavioural boundaries, de-escalate conflict and communicate constructively with participants, families and colleagues. These experiences have reinforced that meaningful engagement often depends on consistency, patience and seeing a person’s strengths beyond their immediate circumstances.

The Salvation Army’s commitment to hope, compassion and dignity strongly aligns with how I want to practise in the AOD sector. I hold a current Working with Children Check and Victorian driver’s licence, have access to a roadworthy vehicle, and completed First Aid and CPR through my Certificate IV in AOD in 2025. I am willing to complete any required police and pre-employment checks.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my lived experience, AOD qualification and transferable community-support skills could contribute to the STAR program.

Yours sincerely,

**Jason Goodwin**
