## Role summary

cohealth is hiring five part-time/full-time fixed-term Alcohol & Other Drugs Harm Reduction Workers in the Melbourne CBD. The role sits in the AOD CBD Response cluster and City Street Health Team.

The position is a hybrid of:

- assertive street outreach;
- drop-in support at 244 Flinders St;
- concierge function;
- Needle & Syringe Program / harm reduction functions.

The PD says the split between functions varies depending on service need and Program Facilitator direction.
