## Verified source facts — 2026-07-14

- CMRH states it supports student learning in community health, mental health, community development, research, advocacy and policy.
- It accepts students from TAFE, universities and colleges, including certificate and diploma students with mandatory placement components.
- CMRH describes supportive supervision and professional development, but says capacity is limited and a suitable placement is not guaranteed.
- Current police check, WWCC and vaccination records are required; criminal-history screening occurs before commencement.
- Students may apply online or through their TAFE/university coordinator; page says staff may respond within two to three weeks.
