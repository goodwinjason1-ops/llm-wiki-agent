## Core placement requirements visible in the handout

The student must perform activities outlined in the performance criteria of these units during **160 hours of work**.

Visible requirements include:

- Work collaboratively with **at least 3 different people with mental illness**, and as relevant their support network, to develop and implement a recovery plan.
- Work with **at least 4 complexity issues** with those people. Visible examples:
  - alcohol and other drugs (AOD)
  - gambling
  - torture
  - grief and loss
  - disability
  - family violence
  - child protection
  - justice system
  - social housing / homelessness
- Establish and maintain self-directed recovery relationships with **at least 3 people with mental illness**.
- Collaborate with **at least 3 different people with mental illness** to develop, implement and review a recovery plan using recovery-oriented approaches.
- Work collaboratively with **at least 3 different people with mental illness** to meet recovery goals.

## WHS tasks visible in the handout

The student must complete WHS tasks at least once in line with state/territory WHS regulations, relevant codes of practice and workplace procedures, including:

- Conduct a workplace risk assessment and record results, including:
  - identification of hazards and potential hazards
  - risk assessment
  - evaluation of policy/procedure in line with state/territory legislation and industry guidelines
  - development of risk controls and measures
- Conduct consultative activities with at least two workers:
  - information session about workplace policies and procedures, including PPE where required for the job role
  - consultative discussion regarding outcomes of a workplace risk assessment, including risk controls and measures developed as part of the risk assessment process
  - monitor workplace compliance with WHS procedures
  - develop a WHS action plan, including strategies for monitoring and review
  - coordinate workplace procedures for a simulated emergency situation

## Unit skill/knowledge headings visible

### CHCMHS002 — Establish self-directed recovery relationships

During placement the student must demonstrate skills and knowledge to:

1. Promote principles of recovery and recovery-oriented practice.
2. Establish the context for a self-directed recovery relationship.
3. Invite information sharing.
4. Define and confirm the collaborative relationship.

### CHCMHS003 — Provide recovery-oriented mental health services

During placement the student must demonstrate skills and knowledge to:

1. Share and collect information to collaboratively inform the plan for recovery.
2. Facilitate collaborative planning process for recovery.
3. Collaboratively implement plan for recovery.
4. Develop and maintain effective working relationships with care support network.
5. Support person during challenges.
6. Collaboratively review the effectiveness of the plan and support provided.

### CHCMHS004 — Work collaboratively with the care network and other services

Visible items include:

1. Identify and build resilience and capacity in the person's care network and community of choice.
2. Develop and maintain effective working relationships with other services and programs.
3. Review and monitor services provided by other organisations and programs.

## Implications for the job finder agent

Prioritise roles/placements where Jayse can demonstrate:

- direct work with people experiencing mental illness;
- recovery planning / care planning / goal setting;
- collaborative work with support networks and other services;
- complexity exposure, especially AOD, homelessness/social housing, justice system, family violence, disability, gambling, grief/loss;
- WHS/risk assessment or policy/procedure participation;
- supervision by qualified mental health/AOD/community-services staff.

## Roles most likely to satisfy placement evidence

High priority:

- Mental Health Placement Student / Student Placement.
- Psychosocial Recovery Support Worker / Mental Health Support Worker.
- AOD/Mental Health Support Worker.
- Dual Diagnosis Support Worker.
- Alcohol and Other Drugs Case Worker / Care and Recovery Support.
- Residential Rehabilitation Support Worker.
- Withdrawal Unit Support Worker / Residential Withdrawal Worker.
- Homelessness + AOD/MH case support.
- Youth AOD / youth mental-health support where adult MH placement criteria can still be met.

Use caution / confirm with Chisholm before relying on:

- Purely observational internships.
- Pure peer/lived-experience roles without recovery plans/care coordination exposure.
- Disability-only support roles with no mental-health recovery planning.
- General community support roles without evidence of direct mental-health work.
- NSP-only / harm-reduction outreach roles if they lack recovery planning/care-network collaboration evidence.
