Re: Application for Harm Reduction Worker - City Street Health Team / 244 Flinders Street

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the Harm Reduction Worker position with cohealth. I hold a Certificate IV in Alcohol and Other Drugs, am one semester from completing a Diploma of Mental Health, and bring decades of lived experience of addiction, treatment and recovery. Together, these give me formal grounding and an authentic, non-judgmental understanding of the people the City Street Health Team supports.

My lived experience includes sustained contact with AOD services, successful completion of pharmacotherapy, and firsthand experience of the stigma, criminalisation and health and social harms associated with substance use. I understand how trauma, mental ill health, unstable housing and rough sleeping, an unregulated drug supply, overdose risk, blood-borne viruses, and barriers to treatment can compound one another. I also understand the practical value of assertive outreach, drop-in support, Needle and Syringe Programs, naloxone and overdose education, safer-use information, advocacy, referral, and respectful service navigation.

My AOD and mental health studies have developed my foundation in person-centred assessment, risk and safety considerations, collaborative goal setting, care planning, brief intervention and referral. I would bring that knowledge within scope, seek guidance appropriately, and work closely with the multidisciplinary team to help clients identify priorities and navigate cohealth and external services, including housing, health, social inclusion, treatment, recovery and pharmacotherapy supports.

As Coach Coordinator with Emerald Lakers Basketball Club, including the Little Lakers youth program, I build trust with young people, families, coaches and volunteers from varied backgrounds. I coordinate group activities and busy community events, communicate clearly, and respond calmly and constructively when situations become challenging. My earlier eleven-year career as a Network Engineer with HP, Telstra, Accenture and Google further demonstrates reliability, documentation, teamwork and composed problem-solving under pressure.

cohealth's commitment to human rights, health equity and person-centred care strongly aligns with my values. I believe people who use drugs deserve safe, confidential and trauma-informed support that respects autonomy, reduces stigma and meets people where they are. I currently hold a valid Working with Children Check and understand the Nationally Coordinated Criminal History Check and Category A immunisation requirements. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my suitability and lived experience openly and respectfully.

Thank you for considering my application.

Kind regards,  
Jason Goodwin
