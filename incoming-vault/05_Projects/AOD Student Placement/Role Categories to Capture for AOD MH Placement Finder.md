## Fit-test questions for every lead

1. Can Jayse complete **160 hours** across Tue/Wed/Fri/Sun?
2. Is the role/placement supervised and suitable for a **Diploma of Mental Health** student?
3. Does it involve direct work with at least **3 people with mental illness**?
4. Can Jayse contribute to recovery plans/goals/care-network collaboration?
5. Does it expose at least 4 complexity issues, ideally including AOD, homelessness, justice, family violence, disability, gambling, grief/loss?
6. Can it provide WHS/risk assessment evidence or workplace policy/procedure activities?
7. Are required checks realistic: WWCC held, Police Check willing, NDIS only if host provides Employer ID/Provider ID?
8. Does it require qualifications Jayse does not yet hold, e.g. completed diploma, degree, AHPRA, social-work registration, years of paid casework?
