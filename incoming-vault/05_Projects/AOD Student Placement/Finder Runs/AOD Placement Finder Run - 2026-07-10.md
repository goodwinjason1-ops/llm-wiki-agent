## Placement-first leads

### 1. ACSO — Student Placements
- **Link:** https://www.acso.org.au/work-at-acso/student-placements/
- **Fit score:** 5
- **Why it fits:** ACSO explicitly hosts tertiary student placements and names AOD-relevant programs: COATS works with people with AOD concerns and court-ordered AOD assessment/treatment; FACT provides AOD assessments and treatment interventions for clients on bail. Activities include treatment plans, casework notes, screening, shadowing case workers, report writing, assessment, referral, and therapeutic approaches.
- **Route:** ACSO says it typically engages students directly through tertiary institution partnerships. Contact listed: `studentplacement@acso.org.au`.
- **Blockers/checks:** Course/provider and placement requirements needed; placement availability limited; likely institution sign-off required. Need Jayse's course name, placement hours, dates, insurance, and coordinator details.

### 2. Turning Point — Educational Internships
- **Link:** https://www.turningpoint.org.au/education/programs/internships
- **Fit score:** 4
- **Why it fits:** 14-week, up to 150 hours, 10–12 hours/week, built specifically as AOD sector introduction for eligible tertiary students. Includes VET units, AOD service presentations, supervision/reflective practice, and site visits.
- **Limit:** Page states it is educational and observational and does **not** offer direct client work. Jayse must confirm whether this can satisfy his placement subject or only supports future applications.
- **Route:** Currently enrolled tertiary students should contact their Placement Coordinator first and lodge an EOI through them plus program instructions.
- **Blockers/checks:** Police Check, WWCC, immunisation history statement, vaccination protocols; Australian citizen/permanent resident eligibility noted; transport/technology costs.
- **Current signal:** page points to a Student Open Night for internships in 2027.

### 3. Odyssey Victoria — Student Placements
- **Link:** https://odyssey.org.au/work-with-us/student-placements/
- **Fit score:** 3 today / 5 when open
- **Why it fits:** Strong AOD placement host; clinically focused placements; historically includes residential rehab, children/families, young people, and complex long-term AOD histories.
- **Current status:** Jina check of the source page says current student placement opportunities are closed. Opportunities are advertised in April/May for second half of year and October/November for first half of following year.
- **Route:** Apply only through currently listed placement opportunities when open; completed Academic Institution/Agency Placement Agreement required on commencement.
- **Blockers/checks:** Police Check, WWCC; mandatory course placement only; qualifications vary by site/program. Some roles may require licence (e.g. outreach/Kids in Focus).
