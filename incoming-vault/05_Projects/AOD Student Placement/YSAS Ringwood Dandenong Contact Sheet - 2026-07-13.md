## Call script

> Hi, my name is Jason Goodwin. I’m completing a Diploma of Mental Health at Chisholm and need a 160-hour supervised placement. I’m calling to ask whether your site is currently considering placement students and who I should email my résumé, placement requirements and availability to. I’m available Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday.

Ask for:

- name and role of placement contact;
- direct email address;
- current capacity;
- supervisor requirements;
- checks and documents required;
- whether Chisholm placement agreements are accepted;
- whether the site can support the required mental-health recovery competencies.
