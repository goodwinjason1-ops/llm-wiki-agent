## Persona review

### Contrarian
- Why might this fail?
- What assumption is weakest?
- What would falsify the proposal?

### Buyer
- Would the owner pay attention to this problem now?
- Is the deliverable understandable in one sentence?
- What would make the arrangement feel safe?

### Researcher
- What baseline is available?
- What comparable process or market evidence exists?
- Which claims are unsupported?

### Operator
- Can the business maintain the output after handover?
- Who approves changes?
- What happens when the source data is stale or contradictory?

- Decision: GO / RESHAPE / PARK / KILL

## Mandatory gate

A pilot cannot become a public case study until scope, data handling, attribution, testimonial/review use and publication permissions are written and approved.
