# Objective

Drive our own traffic via LinkedIn/TikTok content rather than relying solely on Google SEO and referrals. Test whether consistent content creation can generate enquiries from new markets.

# Hypothesis

Consistent LinkedIn posts showing specific transformations (before/after, problem/solution) will generate enquiries from business owners 40+ who recognize their own challenges in the examples.

# Platform Selection

## LinkedIn (primary, 30-day experiment)
**Why:**
- Target audience (business owners 40+) active on LinkedIn
- B2B context fits naturally
- Long-form content allowed (case studies, detailed examples)
- Professional tone matches brand

**Why not TikTok:**
- Target demographic less active there
- Video creation overhead
- B2B services don't typically convert from TikTok

## Content Strategy (if LinkedIn experiment succeeds)
- Add TikTok/Instagram as secondary channel
- Repurpose LinkedIn posts as video scripts
- Test shorter, visual content

# Content Format

## Post structure (adapted from KDP video format)

**Hook (first 3 seconds = first 2 lines):**
- Start with recognizable pain point
- Use specific, concrete language
- Examples:
  - "A financial advisor was spending 3 hours/week answering the same questions"
  - "This wholesale business had 300 SKUs in 6 different spreadsheets"
  - "The youth sports club couldn't find player development data"

**Body (the transformation):**
- Show the "before" state (scattered, manual, frustrating)
- Briefly describe the system built (1-2 sentences)
- Show the "after" state (organized, automated, measurable)

**Specific metrics:**
- Time saved (e.g., "3 hours/week → 30 minutes/week")
- Error reduction (e.g., "15% quote correction rate → 3%")
- Decision confidence (e.g., "12+ calls/week for answers → weekly dashboard")

**Call-to-action:**
- Soft CTA: "If this sounds familiar, we map businesses like yours"
- Link to website contact form
- No hard sell

## Post frequency
- 3x per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday mornings)
- Each post focuses on different vertical:
  - Monday: Consulting/professional services
  - Wednesday: Trade/supply businesses
  - Friday: Community/programs

## Visual elements
- Screenshots of before/after (anonymized)
- Simple diagrams (scattered → structured)
- Metrics visualization (before vs after)
- No generic stock photos

# Case Study Template

Standardize pilot results into reusable cases:

```
BEFORE STATE:
- Hours per week spent on [task]: X
- Error rate / rework rate: X%
- Customer complaints / missed opportunities: X per week
- Tools/systems used: [list]

SYSTEM BUILT:
- Name: [Business Context Brain / Quote Intelligence / etc.]
- Scope: [what it covers]
- Time to build: [days/weeks]
- Key deliverables: [structured data, workflows, dashboards]

AFTER STATE (30 days):
- Hours per week saved: X
- Error rate: X%
- New metrics: [what changed]

CLIENT QUOTE:
"[Specific, not generic praise]"

WHAT CHANGED:
- Time savings
- Error reduction  
- Decision confidence
- Scalability
```

Create this template **before pilots complete**. When pilots finish, fill in immediately.

# Success Metrics

## 30-day targets
- 20+ LinkedIn connections in target industries (consulting, trade, community)
- 3+ enquiries from LinkedIn (tracked via UTM or self-reported "saw on LinkedIn")
- 1+ pilot opportunity from content
- 5+ meaningful engagement (comments, DMs with questions)

## Content quality signals
- Average post engagement (likes + comments) >10 per post
- At least 2 posts with >25 engagement (viral potential)
- DMs asking "how does this work?" (problem-solution fit)

## Conversion metrics
- Click-through rate from LinkedIn to website >2%
- Contact form submission rate from LinkedIn traffic >5%

# Measurement

## Tracking setup
- UTM parameters on all LinkedIn post links
- Google Analytics: segment "LinkedIn" traffic
- Weekly content performance review (which posts resonated?)

## A/B testing (if baseline established)
- Test different hook formats (question vs statement)
- Test CTA strength (soft vs direct)
- Test visual style (screenshots vs diagrams)

# Gates

## Before scaling
- 30-day experiment shows >2 enquiries from LinkedIn
- Content feels authentic (not "growth hacking")
- Sustainable (3x/week is manageable)

## Before paid promotion
- Organic posts show clear engagement patterns
- We understand which verticals respond
- Cost-per-enquiry baseline established organically

# NOT Implementing
- Paid LinkedIn ads until organic proves out
- Video content until text-based posts validated
- Cross-posting to other platforms until LinkedIn works
- Claiming revenue increases without causation proof

# Relationship to Pilot Strategy

This content strategy supports the pilot arrangement:
- Pilots provide case studies for content
- Content generates new pilot opportunities
- Both feed into external traffic → brand recognition → enquiries

# Decision

Start 30-day LinkedIn experiment. Commit to 3x/week posting for August 2026. Evaluate success metrics at day 14 and day 30. If >2 enquiries from LinkedIn, continue and consider scaling. If 0 enquiries, reassess platform choice.
