# Demo purpose

Show a prospective small business how scattered operational material becomes a usable decision system without using real client data.

# Demo sequence

1. **Messy sources** — sample inbox excerpts, product PDF, quote spreadsheet and process note, all synthetic.
2. **Source register** — record source, owner, date, confidence and permitted use.
3. **Context map** — connect customer type, offer, process step, evidence and decision.
4. **Decision view** — answer three bounded questions: what is known, what is missing, and what action is safe next.
5. **Handover** — show the maintenance rule, review cadence and human approval points.

# Synthetic example

- Business: wholesale doors/windows supplier (not a client claim).
- Problem: quote information spread across a product PDF, price sheet and old email.
- Output: product/specification index, quote-review checklist and exception log.
- Measurement: time-to-find information and correction count only; no promised revenue result.
