## Key takeaways for us

- Reusable video/source ingestion should be a product capability, not just a manual research step.
- A Business Context Brain should turn videos into durable notes, workflows, and source-linked summaries.
- Service-first one-person businesses can be launched by using AI to compress research, build, and delivery.
- The offer must be narrow and buyer-facing, not generic AI automation.
- Reddit/communities can be used to find painful business problems and language customers already use.
- Business Context Brain prospecting can mine phrases like documentation chaos, onboarding mess, SOP chaos, quote templates, and lost context.
- Digital products work best when built from repeated pain, reusable templates, and a clear transformation.
- Business Context Brain outputs can become internal digital products: SOP packs, quote libraries, onboarding packs, training packs.
