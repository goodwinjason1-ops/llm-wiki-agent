## Synthesis for Quant Floor design

Antoine's recent channel corpus is not a single clean strategy; it is a high-risk discretionary/on-chain operating style with repeated components:

1. **Early discovery:** find new coins, airdrops, narrative shifts, and tool edges before they become obvious.
2. **Wallet intelligence:** watch wallets, whales, smart traders, insiders, copy-trade flows, and repeated actors.
3. **Tool stack arbitrage:** use or compare fast execution/research tools such as BullX, Axiom, Padre, GMGN, Photon, Nova, StalkFun, Polymarket tooling, and airdrop platforms.
4. **Rug/scam avoidance:** focus on bundled supply, suspicious dev wallets, holder concentration, liquidity quality, sniper/bot behavior, and social manipulation.
5. **Small-account asymmetry:** position for high upside while accepting many small losses, but the vault should convert this into explicit bet sizing and kill-switches rather than vibes.
6. **AI-assisted automation:** build agents and bots to scan, rank, summarize, and monitor; keep execution separate until evidence and approvals exist.

## Quant Floor implication

Create a new **Antoine On-Chain Alpha Desk** that sits beside the existing strategy desks. Its role is not to blindly trade memecoins; it should transform Antoine-style qualitative/on-chain ideas into a gated pipeline:

`Source/Wallet/Tool Signal → Rug/Risk Gate → Narrative/Flow Score → Paper Bet Sizing → Outcome Ledger → Review Board`
