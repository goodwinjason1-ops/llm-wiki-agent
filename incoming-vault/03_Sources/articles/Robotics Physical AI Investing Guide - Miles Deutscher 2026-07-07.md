# Robotics Physical AI Investing Guide - Miles Deutscher 2026-07-07

## Explicit source thesis

- Robotics / physical AI is framed as a multi-year asymmetric theme, not a quick trade.
- Constraints described as breaking: AI capability and hardware/manufacturing cost.
- Five exposure layers are proposed: ETFs, large-cap public equities, pick-and-shovels, pure-play robotics stocks, and high-risk betas.

## Alpha hypotheses

1. Robotics is a long-horizon thematic sleeve, not a short-term signal.
2. A raw robotics basket may have strong upside but unacceptable drawdowns.
3. A positive-momentum / volatility-target overlay may express the theme more safely than static exposure.
4. Benchmark against QQQ/SPY to avoid confusing high-beta tech exposure with alpha.
