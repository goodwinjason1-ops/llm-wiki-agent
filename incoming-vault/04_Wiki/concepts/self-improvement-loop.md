# Self-Improvement Loop

The self-improvement loop turns repeated tasks into durable assets: after doing a task, capture what worked, convert it into a workflow or skill, use it next time, then patch the skill when it fails or improves. Hermes skills and Obsidian workflow notes are the two storage layers.
